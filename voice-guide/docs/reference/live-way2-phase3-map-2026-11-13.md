A read-only map made before phase 3 of the live plan (`docs/handoff/live-demos-way2.md` at the repository root), by seven readers (Pocket core, portal, journey host, storage and ids on Opus; actors, tests, build and wiring on Sonnet) and an Opus cross-check. Facts carry file:line as of commit e2d8628; probes ran on the saved pages of 2026-10-23 in Playwright Chromium with scripts stripped and no network. "Verified" means read or run; the rest is inferred. Owner answers to its questions are recorded below. This file is a snapshot: the code moves on.

## Unsafe on a live page as built (before phase 3)

- Opening the Pocket throws on a null `.page-shell` (pocket.js:187, 338) as an unhandled rejection, and state.busy stays true. When the shell does exist, a sticker with `image: null` (all 20 bundles) throws at pocket.js:706 after #main-content has already been hidden (pocket.js:840-842), so Vodafone's content stays hidden with no way back.
- The album is appended inside #main-content (pocket.js:273), and the page turn hides it along with #main-content (pocket.js:819-822, 840).
- 'Pocket it' puts a <button> inside <a class=ticket> cards and does not preventDefault (pocket.js:676-680), so a click navigates away (Cash 8/8, bundles 3/20).
- The chip, liner and hover-lift clone are written inside Vodafone's cards (pocket.js:449, 667-689) without data-aixc. SKIP lacks [data-aixc] (recognisers.js:106). Hovering renames ids (mo-salah-world → fkwenp1q3ubi-1) and the Vodafone Cash band loses its tag, which breaks the guide's spotlight and the trail's lookups.
- No Pocket, actor, portal or journey node carries data-aixc, and destroy() removes no layers, chips, classes, body attributes or listeners (pocket.js:947-977). A guard stop cannot leave the page as Vodafone served it.
- Handlers, rAF loops and timers run outside the guard (pocket.js 17 listeners; actors/scope.js has no catch). Errors reach Vodafone's window and, inferred, its Dynatrace monitoring.
- pocket.css:291-293 permanently replaces the widget's `transition: all .25s cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)` with `transition: bottom 0.28s ease` on #vodafone-aw-icon, even when nothing is lifted. That changes Vodafone's widget outside the owner's exception.
- The lift (pocket.css:295-298) can move the button onto the Arabic OneTrust banner (bottom-right). The widget's host paints at the top z-index, so the button covers the cookie banner (rule 4.6).
- Portal: it intercepts clicks on the whole document (interceptor.js:61-85), which hijacks Vodafone's header and footer links (73/74 on Services). The proxy is made with cloneNode(false), so `onclick="footerHandler(event)"` runs twice (handoff.js:168-180). The wall copies `<accessibility-widget>`, re-running Vodafone's Angular widget, and #onetrust-consent-sdk, showing two banners (wall.js:30, 131-137). Each same-origin click makes an extra iframe request to Vodafone. The `destination.scripts` option would run Vodafone's theme in a same-origin frame, with its clear() and analytics (config.js:133-139, handoff.js:115). `html{overflow:hidden !important}` stays after a guard stop (portal.css:16-18). A failed start leaves `pending` set and swallows every later link click (portal.js:110-113). Escape navigates on to the destination, which can be a 'Buy now' page (controller.js:481-487).
- Assets loaded through import.meta.url 404 on the gateway (server.ts:103-113): portal.css (an unstyled scene is appended to body while html gets overflow hidden), mascot.svg and drone.svg (the actors silently vanish), tips.css and world.css.
- Storage: the theme's Guest Case wipes 'aixc.pocket.v1' on home, Services, RED and Cash. The first write on another page deletes the earlier pages' stickers (pocket.js:196, 205-211).
- Trail: the consented pill navigates to /{lang}/services#journey, where nothing hosts the journey (controller.ts:367-371, 510), so it is a dead pill. journey.js:503 rejects any host that is not version 1. On live, `voiceGuide.trailHost.allow()` lets any script on Vodafone's page, third-party tags included, grant trail consent without a press (controller.ts:569, 583-584).
- The clone's wiring must not reach live: the composer stores a Gemini key in Vodafone's localStorage and posts page content to Google (aixc/controller.js:24, 55; phase 4 is templates only), and main.js:53-55 writes html[data-vg-trail].
- Each page turn clones the page twice and switches lazy images to eager (pocket.js:340; curl.js:235-236): about 5.4k nodes and extra image downloads from Vodafone per turn. Inferred: Vodafone's accessibility widget may process the clones.

## Owner decisions and answers

### OD-1 (plan D2): When do 'Pocket it' chips show on Vodafone's live pages?

- Options: Always visible, as on the clone; On hover and focus on desktop, always on phones (the plan's recommendation); On hover and focus only, with a small tab on phones; Off until a 'Pocket mode' switch is turned on
- Recommendation: On hover and focus on desktop and always on phones, with the chip never placed over a card's title.
- Why: Always-visible chips overlap Vodafone's titles: H4 on all 7 Great Entertainment cards and all 4 home horizontal cards, an H3 on a bundle card, and the H2 of the Cash band on phones (pocket-core probe5). That would be 30 extra buttons in the tab order on Services. This is a choice about the look of Vodafone's real page (rule 4.8). Plan §4 lists D2 as blocking phase 3.
- Blocks: S6 (chips) and every test that clicks a chip
- **Answer:** Always visible, but out of the keyboard tab order (owner, 2026-11-13).

### OD-2: May a sticker stored in the visitor's browser, or the committed demo kit, keep page values such as a bundle's price?

- Options: Store the copy as published, labelled with its capture date ('37 EGP on 12 Nov'); Keep the price out of the stored record; show it only when the card is on the current page, otherwise link to it; Store no text beyond the title and read the rest live
- Recommendation: Keep the price out of the stored record and out of the demo kit. The adapter folds the price into `description` (live-adapter.js:262-266), so store the price as a separate field that is never persisted, and show it only from the live card.
- Why: 47 of 155 saved cards carry a price in their title or description. Rule 4.3 names the knowledge base, traces and logs, not the visitor's own device. ADR-0037 keeps replays that show prices on the device under consent. The demo kit would be committed to the repository (plan 3.6). Only the owner can set the scope of rule 4.3.
- Blocks: S4 (record format and ADR-0051), and phase 7's demo kit
- **Answer:** Store the copy as published, price included, labelled with its capture date; the visitor's browser and the committed demo kit are outside AGENTS 4.3 (owner, 2026-11-13).

### OD-3: What does a sticker open on live pages, and which clicks get the portal transition?

- Options: The sticker opens its source page with the card spotlighted, through the portal; Vodafone's own CTA stays a plain native link; only stickers get the portal; Vodafone's card CTA as today: 'Subscribe' → /spa/miManagement (17), 'Buy now' → eshop (19), /spa/balanceTransfer, and the Arabic 'Subscribe now' link to www2.vf-live.com/VFHEProxy (a header-enrichment subscription); No portal on live in phase 3: stickers link out natively
- Recommendation: The first option. Vodafone's header and footer links (73-74 on Services) are never intercepted, and nothing animates into a commit flow.
- Why: Today the listener is on the whole document (interceptor.js:61-85), so it catches Vodafone's own navigation while the album is open. Sticker CTAs lead into subscribe and buy flows. Pressing them is the person's own click, but the demo would animate people into commit flows on Vodafone's real site (rule 4.1 territory). This is a product choice.
- Blocks: S13 (portal on live, ADR-0054)
- **Answer:** Owner asked whether the frame's response can be reused for the real navigation (2026-11-13). Portal on sticker clicks only; Vodafone's own links stay native (recommendation, not contradicted).

### OD-4: For same-origin destinations on live, does the portal frame the real page or show a preview?

- Options: Frame, as today: one extra document request to Vodafone per click (Vodafone's server and CDN count a second page view); raw server HTML with 27 of 34 images without src and Owl hidden, so a visible seam at handoff; Preview always on live: no extra request, and no claim of a seamless last frame; Frame only an allow-list of pages the owner has checked live
- Recommendation: Preview on live by default. Keep framing as a pilot setting for the owner's own demo, only after he has looked at it live. Frame scripts stay off in every case.
- Why: Portal probes E1 and E4: the frame makes a request the visitor did not make, to Vodafone's real site, and probably skews their analytics. ADR-0005 recorded 'last frame = destination' on rendered saved pages only.
- Blocks: S13
- **Answer:** One fetch of the destination, drawn in a script-free frame, then a navigation served from the HTTP cache (Vodafone HTML: Cache-Control max-age=14400, public, measured on 12 Nov responses); proven by a request-counting test, else a picture preview with no extra request.

### OD-5: Once hostUrl is gone, where does 'Your journey' open on pages where the layer cannot host it: /spa/ pages, eshop, ?aixc=off, the journey switched off, or a layer that failed?

- Options: The guide's built-in sheet (ADR-0038), the look ADR-0040 removed from Vodafone pages; No journey there: the pill only handles consent, or is hidden; Keep a hostUrl-like setting and navigate to a page that hosts
- Recommendation: The built-in sheet as the fallback, so there is never a dead pill. Record it in ADR-0050.
- Why: view.ts:497 and controller.ts:510 treat hostUrl as 'hosted', so the sheet never shows today. On live, the pill navigates to /{lang}/services#journey, where nothing hosts it. Which look appears on Vodafone's pages without the layer is a product choice.
- Blocks: S10 (hostUrl removal) and the fallback test
- **Answer:** The guide's built-in sheet (owner, 2026-11-13).

## Engineering decisions (to be recorded in the ADRs named)

### Where does the album live, and what does the page turn freeze?

- Choice: The album gets its own region inserted by PageHost after #experience-output, marked data-aixc and data-vg and keeping id #pocket-output so pocket.css still matches. A separate region is needed because the tuck-in keys on `#experience-output:not([hidden])`. #main-content is hidden only through host.hideSource() and host.showSource() (page-host.js:80-87), never by setting node.hidden. The turn snapshots `#wrapper` (falling back to body), clipped below host.chromeBottom(), and makes one clone per turn: seeThrough 0 on live, or a shared fill (curl.js:230-241). The clone strips ids and every data-aixc-* attribute. The Pocket takes these seams: snapshotRoot, pocketParent, hideSource, showSource, chromeBottom and guard; shell.js supplies the clone's equivalents.
- Why: Today the album is appended inside main (pocket.js:273) and hidden by its own turn (819-822, 840). `.page-shell` is null on live, so the turn throws (187, 338). Two clones made 5,416 nodes on bundles. The seams keep the clone path working until phase 9.
- ADR: ADR-0052 (Pocket on live pages)

### Where are the 'Pocket it' chips, the pocketed state and the hover lift drawn?

- Choice: In an overlay layer outside #main-content (data-aixc data-vg), positioned from each [data-aixc-item] box. Chips are hidden when the card has zero size or is clipped by an overflow ancestor (the Owl stage). The pocketed liner is drawn in the overlay, and the hover lift goes in a fixed layer, never `card.append` (pocket.js:449). Chips are never inside an <a>. They are re-keyed by element on each `aixc:model` event (index.js:100). For the keyboard, focus entering a tagged card shows its chip and Tab hands over to it.
- Why: This follows ADR-0046 §2 and plan 3.3 (Vodafone's content is only tagged). It removes the nested <button> in <a> (Cash 8/8 and bundles 3/20 navigated away), and it stops the re-reads and id renames our own nodes caused. The cost is tracking scroll, resize, ResizeObserver and Owl motion. Owl motion cannot be tested offline.
- ADR: ADR-0052

### How does the adapter treat the layer's own nodes, and what does it expose for re-matching?

- Choice: Add `[data-aixc]` to SKIP in recognisers.js:106; NOT_CARDS and the pressable count inherit it. Expose `item.keys` (a list of {kind, value}: the link path without the language, the picture file, the title, the tab pane, the digits in the title) and `item.kind` on the model. normalizeContentModel keeps unknown item fields (content-index.js:47-55).
- Why: This one line fixes the Cash band losing its tag and the 'mo-salah-world' → 'fkwenp1q3ubi-1' rename. The keys make AIXC-41 re-matching possible: the storage map measured 69 of 69 matches across dates and none wrong across languages.
- ADR: ADR-0049 amendment

### Where does the Pocket store its records on live (O23)?

- Choice: Its own IndexedDB database `aixc-v1`, with an in-memory mirror for the first paint, a BroadcastChannel to sync tabs, and memory when IndexedDB does not open. The `aixc.pocket.v1` id array is migrated once. The store is never filtered by the current page. No Storage.prototype patch, and no navigator.storage.persist() on Vodafone's origin.
- Why: IndexedDB survived the Guest Case clear in Chromium, and no theme script touches it. It sidesteps the race between the read and the clear. Plan 3.9 allows IndexedDB.
- ADR: ADR-0051 (Pocket store and sticker identity)

### What does a Pocket record hold, and how does a sticker find its card again?

- Choice: Each record has its own key. It holds the display copy with its capture language, the image {src, alt} or null, the cta {label, href, opens}, the card kind, and all keys with their kinds. It records the source page (path without /en or /ar, the KB page id when the page map answered, and the page title per language), the section id and title, savedAt, lastSeenAt and origin (person or kit). The price follows OD-2. Re-matching is scored with a threshold of 4 (3 only with a unique tab pane), and the stored id is refreshed on a match. A sticker whose card is gone stays, labelled 'No longer on this page', but only after a read with coverage above the threshold. The album groups by the language-neutral path and names groups in the current language.
- Why: Today the store keeps ids only and drops ids from other pages (pocket.js:196, 205-211). 74 of 155 items would rename if a card sharing their link were added. Only 2 of 20 bundle ids match across languages.
- ADR: ADR-0051

### What loads when, and what are the budgets?

- Choice: The core gains `ensureCss(name, text)` (a `<style data-aixc data-vg>` in head), a demo loader through guard.run (`import('./demos/pocket.js')`), the trail claim stub and a check of `history.state.vfPortal`. The Pocket chunk (chips, peek, curl, album, journey dock) loads at idle only where the Pocket is offered: coverage ≥ 0.6, stored stickers, or a needed claim. The actors and the portal scene are dynamic imports on first open and first sticker click. Every manifest entry gets a budget keyed on `entry`, and the core budget is raised on purpose with a measured figure. Splitting a chips-only chunk out, to honour plan 3.10 fully, is a measured follow-up.
- Why: The core has 608 B left. initPocket's decorate() sits inside an 800-line closure. The actors are 47 KB gzipped and the portal controller 15 KB, and the clone creates both at init (pocket.js:277-298).
- ADR: ADR-0052, plus an ADR-0048 amendment (budgets)

### How do CSS and the `new URL('./x', import.meta.url)` assets reach a live page?

- Choice: A buildAixc plugin rewrites `new URL('./x.(css|svg)', import.meta.url)` in portal.js:42, pocket-actors.js:66-67, tips/entry.js:42 and personalization/index.js:38 into data: URLs, so the modules and the clone stay unchanged. The live-only demo entries import pocket.css, journey.css, pocket-actors.css and pocket-cursor.css as text for ensureCss, minified through esbuild's CSS transform (−5.5 KB gzipped). Any <link> or <style> the layer adds carries data-aixc. A test asserts that no `new URL("./` remains in the output.
- Why: The gateway serves only `^[\w.-]+\.js(\.map)?$` (server.ts:104), so these files 404 today. The actors' fetch failure is swallowed (pocket-actors.js:2050). None of these CSS files uses url(), so data: URLs are safe. ADR-0048 §3 promised this plugin.
- ADR: ADR-0048 §3 amendment

### How do the Pocket, the actors, the portal and the journey fit the guard?

- Choice: Each takes the guard. Listeners go through guard.listen; timers, rAF, observers and tween callbacks through guard.wrap, with an onError in actors/scope.js. Every node carries data-aixc and data-vg. open and close clear busy, call showSource and remove layers in a `finally`. destroy() removes everything and is registered with guard.onStop. The first error stops the whole layer, as ADR-0048 §7 says. New test checkpoints: pocket-open, pocket-render, pocket-close, actors-frame and portal-start.
- Why: pocket.js has 17 raw listeners and destroy() only stops the actors and the portal (947-977). The probes saw unhandled rejections reach the page and #main-content left hidden.
- ADR: ADR-0052

### What replaces `position: sticky` on Vodafone's static themes?

- Choice: Fixed positioning, bounded by JS (an IntersectionObserver on the album's end), for the actors' floor (pocket-actors.css:147) and the journey band (journey.css:40). Never change Vodafone's html or body overflow.
- Why: static-theme and static-lite set `body,html{overflow-x:hidden}`, which makes body the sticky container, and the actors map's probe showed the floor not sticking. maintheme does not set it, so test both a static-theme page and /home.
- ADR: ADR-0052

### Which side and which fonts in Arabic?

- Choice: The corner, peek, lift and actors stay bottom-right in both languages. The Pocket and journey font stacks add Vodafone's -ar families under :lang(ar) or [dir=rtl]. Sticker copy carries the lang and dir it was captured in.
- Why: The widget's button is `right:20px` on the Arabic page (capture). pocket.css and journey.css name only the -en families.
- ADR: ADR-0052

### What shape does the run-time trail-host claim take?

- Choice: A single owner: `claim()` returns `release()`; a second claimant gets null. The static meta counts as a permanent claim until phase 9. `hosted` becomes a getter used by every reader (controller.ts:366, 498, 510, 585, 1011, 1014, 1042). A claim made while the built-in sheet is open hands over (collapse, then `vg:trail-open`). A release collapses the view, destroys the preview and clears #hostRoot. The core claims on `vg:trail-ready` when the pocket and journey demos are on, and releases through guard.onStop.
- Why: `readonly hosted` (controller.ts:136) cannot change after construction. The RED world revamp only reads. An unreleased claim leaves a dead pill.
- ADR: ADR-0050 (trail host interface v2)

### Which step is selected when the journey opens in place on a live page?

- Choice: The current page when a live host has claimed. The 'previous page' rule (controller.ts:1010-1011) stays only for the meta host on the clone.
- Why: That rule exists because the Services clone was not a step of interest. On live, the page the person is on is a real step.
- ADR: ADR-0050

### How does the trail keep consent through the theme's clear (T-806)?

- Choice: A copy of the consent in IndexedDB, with the localStorage key kept as a cache and a BroadcastChannel replacing storage events for cross-tab sync. The demo browser's 'vg-' guard stays for replay only.
- Why: Consent lives in trail.js (trail/consent.ts), not guide.js, so guide.js's 165 B of headroom is untouched. Not patching Storage.prototype keeps plan 3.8 intact and works in Mode A.
- ADR: ADR-0050 (amends ADR-0037 §1)

### Version handshake, and how exposed the host API is to page scripts

- Choice: journey.js:503 accepts `version >= 1` and reads v2 fields only when they are present; the version bump and the journey.js change land in one commit. `ask()` gets the two-letter language fallback. `allow()` and `seed()` require user activation, and `seed()` also requires a demo flag sent by the server. No new globals are added beyond the existing `voiceGuide.trailHost`.
- Why: journey.js rejects any version other than 1, so a bump would break the clone. Any script on Vodafone's page, third-party tags included, can call `allow()` today (controller.ts:569, 583-584).
- ADR: ADR-0050

### Does the Pocket exist on pages where 'Pocket it' coverage is below 0.6?

- Choice: Yes, as an album-only Pocket on every page the layer runs on: no chips, but stickers from other pages and the journey host. Chips appear only where coverage is 0.6 or more.
- Why: Plan 3.5 row 4 says the journey is on every page. wireless-net reads 0 items, and initPocket returns null without a model (pocket.js:188-189).
- ADR: ADR-0052

### How is the accessibility-button lift implemented under the owner's exception?

- Choice: Write the exception into AGENTS.md §4.6 with: the selector #vodafone-aw-icon, the three states, 230/150 px, and the portal's holdLive. Scope the `transition` rule to the lifted states. Neither lift nor show the corner while #onetrust-banner-sdk is visible. Drop the `z-index: 10 !important`, which does nothing on live. The walker test allows exactly these writes. A check proves the button stays clickable and never sits on the banner.
- Why: pocket.css:291-293 changes the widget's own transition permanently. The widget's host paints at the top z-index, so a lifted button covers the Arabic bottom-right banner.
- ADR: ADR-0053 (accessibility-button lift on the owner's live demos)

### How does the portal behave on live, apart from OD-3 and OD-4?

- Choice: The listener sits on the album root. Navigation uses a fresh <a> carrying only href, target, rel and referrerpolicy, so no on* handlers are copied. KEEP_LIVE becomes 'vg-guide, accessibility-widget, #onetrust-consent-sdk'. freeze() stubs every element whose localName customElements.get() knows, and root-level media. destination.scripts is forced off on live. Eligible hosts: the page origin only. The pending state is reset when start throws. Escape cancels on live. .vf-portal and the CSS node carry data-aixc, and html[data-vf-portal] is removed on stop.
- Why: Portal probes: the wall re-ran the widget's constructor (1→2); footer links carry onclick="footerHandler(event)" (handoff.js:168-180); after a stop, `html{overflow:hidden !important}` is left behind (portal.css:16-18).
- ADR: ADR-0054 (portal on live pages; the clone-local ADR-0005 stays for the clone)

### How are the live-page tests built?

- Choice: A new helper, voice-guide/tests/e2e/vodafone-layer.ts. No clone. Image, font and stylesheet routes are stubbed, and a request log asserts nothing reaches web.vodafone.com.eg. Stand-ins come from the capture: an Owl unfold shim plus an Owl-loaded Services variant; the widget in its real shape (a 0×0 fixed host at the top z-index holding button.vf-aw-fab with right 20, bottom 20, 55×55 and `transition: all .25s`); a OneTrust banner and filter at the recorded geometry; a theme-clear stand-in on maintheme and static-theme pages. Viewports 1366×900 and 375×812. One port per file: 4477 pocket-live, 4478 pocket-pages, 4479 pocket-actors, 4480 pocket-portal, 4484 pocket.live, 4485 floating-matrix. Long suites run as `npm run test:e2e:pocket`, with each test() under 120 s. Pixel checks use an in-page canvas. The Python clone suites stay green until phase 9.
- Why: Replay runs no scripts: Owl is display:none (7 of 29 Services cards visible), there is no banner, the widget stand-in has z 1, and there is no clear(). aixc-layer's image stub still lets one font through to the live site.
- ADR: None. Recorded in the handoff §6 and the runbook.

### portal

The portal (experiences/src/pocket/portal/*, clone-local ADR experiences/docs/adr/0005-pocket-portal-transition.md) is a click interceptor plus a lazy 3D scene. It turns a plain click on a same-tab http(s) <a>/<area> into a transition of about 3.3 s, and then does the real navigation by clicking a shallow copy of that link. It runs only while the Pocket album is open (isPocketPage(): state.view === 'pocket' && !state.busy). It listens on the whole document, so it is not limited to stickers. It never touches buttons, forms, javascript:, tel:/mailto:, download links, new-tab or modified clicks, or other hosts. Eligible hosts are this page's origin plus any *.vodafone.com.eg host. At the click it builds a "wall": cloneNode(true) of every visible body child except script/style/link/template/noscript and the KEEP_LIVE selectors 'vg-guide, #vodafone-aw-icon'. It also starts loading the destination in a temporary iframe with sandbox="allow-same-origin" (no scripts) when the destination is same-origin. Cross-origin destinations get a "preview" card built from the sticker's own DOM. A destination counts as ready when it has loaded, its status is under 400 and it has enough content (more than 60 characters of text or more than 1 image); fonts and first-screen images get up to 1.2 s. After that it falls back to the preview: blocked, HTTP error, not enough content, or 3.8 s after the click. History is only replaceState: the current entry is marked {vfPortal:{view:'pocket',scrollY}}. There is no pushState and no popstate listener. A fresh Back reopens the album at once from that mark; a back/forward-cache Back just removes the overlay (pageshow, persisted). Navigation always happens: on arrival, on Escape, on a change of window width, on any error (location.assign), or after 9 s at the latest. If the browser stays on the page, the page is given back after 10 s. holdLive (B-27) pins Vodafone's accessibility button and the guide's --vg-lift inline with !important. It eases them from their lifted place down to bottom 20 px while the destination approaches, and restores them only on teardown. On a live page, one same-origin click makes one extra document request (the iframe) on top of the real navigation; client tags do not run in the frame, but the server sees two page views. The iframe also receives Vodafone's raw server HTML: no lazy-loaded pictures, Owl carousels hidden. The seamless last frame (0 pixels differ), measured only on saved, already rendered pages, will not hold on live pages. Main live problems I verified offline:
- **Header and footer links are caught:** Vodafone's header and footer links, 73–74 of them on Services, would all get the portal while the album shows.
- **The accessibility widget is copied and re-run:** the real accessibility button sits inside an Angular custom element, `<accessibility-widget>`, so the wall copies and re-creates the widget (Vodafone's own widget code runs a second time).
- **The stylesheet is not built:** portal.css is not emitted by the aixc esbuild build, so the overlay would be unstyled.
- **Duplicate handler run:** inline onclick handlers are copied onto the proxy link, so they run twice.
- **Clean-up on failure:** none of the portal's nodes or listeners go through the layer's guard (`data-aixc`, `guard.on`).

### journey-host

The Exploration Trail (trail.js, loaded by guide.js 600 ms after start-up only when site.trail.enabled; guide.ts:164) decides once, in a readonly class field, whether the page hosts the journey: `readonly hosted = !!document.querySelector('meta[name="vg-trail-host"]')` (controller.ts:136). Only the clone's HTML carries that meta (experiences/index.html:13, ar/index.html:14); the demo browser serves those pages unchanged in replay and adds no meta. On a hosted page the consented pill dispatches `vg:trail-open` (controller.ts:366) and the clone's journey.js turns the page to the Pocket and draws the journey through TrailHostApi version 1 (host.ts:36-63), which is published once as `voiceGuide.trailHost` and as the detail of `vg:trail-ready` at the end of start() (controller.ts:581-589). On other pages with `trail.hostUrl`, the pill sets the sessionStorage flag `vg-trail:<siteKey>:open`, flushes and calls `location.assign(hostUrl.replace('{lang}', lang))` (controller.ts:367-371). The host page reads that flag only if it was hosted from the start (controller.ts:585-587). Because render passes `hosted: this.hosted || !!this.settings.hostUrl` (controller.ts:510; view.ts:497), the guide's own sheet never appears on a site that sets hostUrl. On a site with neither, the built-in sheet opens. On live today (no clone) the hostUrl goes to the real /en/services, which has no host, so the pill is a dead end (worked out from the code; the live site could not be reached, O29). ADR-0048 §2 rejects a meta added by the loader: `hosted` cannot change after construction, so a meta would leave a pill that opens nothing whenever the layer is off, has failed or has no Pocket, and failure isolation could not undo it. Phase 3 therefore needs a run-time claim()/release() in the one trail host interface version 2 (ADR-0050). The same version must cover the revamp's asked questions (snapshot().asked, askAbout, questionsFor) and a consent-gated seed() for the demo kit. journey.js is shared by the clone and the live layer, and it rejects any host whose version is not 1 (journey.js:503), so the version bump and the journey.js change must land in one commit. Measured today with the size test's own method, built from HEAD into the scratchpad: guide.js 20,315 B gzipped (165 B left of 20,480), trail.js 27,135 B (3,585 B left of 30,720), and the aixc core 7,072 B (608 B left of 7,680). Version 2 must live in trail.js, never in guide.js. On live pages, trail consent sits in localStorage, which Vodafone's theme clears on load (O23). The demo browser protects `vg-` keys only on pages opened live from a replay, so the journey cannot keep consent on a live run without a change.

### actors

The Pocket actors are two decorative characters, a mascot and a drone, drawn as SVG that JavaScript re-poses every frame. There is no canvas, no WebGL, no three.js and no runtime dependency. A tiny hand-written 3D kit (actors/space.js) poses a model, projects it with perspective and rewrites SVG path and attribute values; the only flat artwork is two decal sheets (mascot.svg, drone.svg). Motion is physics (springs, a drone flight model, a hoverboard that flies on the same model) plus scripted clips. Each actor has one requestAnimationFrame loop owned by a cleanup scope (actors/scope.js); loops stop when hidden, off screen, in a background tab or with reduced motion, and the mascot draws every other frame when only breathing. The controller is createPocketActors({pocketRoot, peekButton, sheet, reducedMotion}) in pocket-actors.js:106. pocket.js builds it in a try/catch (pocket.js:293-298) and calls itemPocketed, itemLanded, openRequested, openStart/Progress/Complete, closeStart/Complete and destroy; every call goes through a try/catch wrapper (pocket-actors.js:2056-2069).

Where it draws. On the page view the mascot lives in a stage inside the curl's "under" layer (peekStage, returned by underStage() and appended in pocket.js:322): the paper clips it, the roll covers it, and its mittens are an SVG inside the pocket button. In the album the drone layer (.pocket-actor-layer, position relative, z-index 3, height 0, pointer-events none) and the mascot floor (.pocket-actor-floor, position sticky, bottom 0, z-index 3) are both appended to the END of #pocket-output (pocket-actors.js:205), so the drone is positioned from the layer's box at the bottom of the album and scrolls with it. The cursor light (pocket-cursor.js) is separate: fixed nodes at z 60, 59 and 2. Only the drone button (pointer-events auto, 44 px minimum) takes input.

What it reads from the page is almost entirely the album's own DOM: .pocket-view__inner (content right edge), .pocket-view__bar, .pocket-back, .pocket-clear, .pocket-view__mark, #pocket-title, .pocket-view__lead, .pocket-empty, .pocket-grid, .pocket-grid__item, .pocket-sticker. Three things come from outside the album: .curl-host--flying (the flying sticker), the three blocker selectors #vodafone-aw-icon, vg-guide and .pocket-peek (pocket-actors.js:1339, used only for the drone's parking spot), and window scroll/resize/innerHeight/clientWidth. It reads no clone selector such as #source-content, .site-header or .page-shell; those appear only in pocket.js and in the Python e2e. It keeps no storage and makes no network call except fetching its two SVG decal sheets (pocket-actors.js:102). It has no RTL logic: it always anchors to the physical right content edge.

Wiring on live is not done yet. The actors are loaded from the clone's index.html (two <link> tags, lines 41 and 43) and main.js; src/host/index.js does not mention them.

I ran the saved live pages to test it. Result: the album floor's `position: sticky` does not stick on Vodafone's pages, so the mascot would not stay at the bottom of the viewport. The theme gives both html and body `overflow: hidden auto`, which makes body the sticky scroll container. This is the biggest finding for the actors. Details are in the facts and risks below.

Sizes. Bundled with esbuild (minified ESM), pocket-actors.js with the cursor is 143,885 bytes and 47.3 KB gzip. That includes src/i18n.js (27 KB source), which the other layer modules will share as a chunk; without it the actors are 97.7 KB minified and 37.9 KB gzip. Add 2.6 KB gzip for pocket-actors.css, 1.6 KB for pocket-cursor.css, and 2.1 KB plus 0.7 KB for the two SVGs. Source total is 271,351 bytes against the 270 KiB test budget (276,480), so about 5 KB of headroom.

Measured cost, in a harness (not the real Pocket): the real actors bundle and the three real stylesheets mounted on saved live pages with a minimal album of 12 cards, headless Chromium at 1366 by 860, 60 fps in every run. Per second, script cost was about 77 to 83 ms and total main-thread task time 150 to 210 ms. That is roughly 1.3 ms of script and 3 ms of task per frame. Layout and style recalculation run every frame (60 per second; layout 6 to 8 ms per second, style 16 to 33 ms per second) because every frame rewrites SVG attributes. At 4x CPU throttling script was about 300 ms per second and total task time 550 to 665 ms per second; frame rate stayed at 60 fps while the pointer moved, and dropped to 52.7 fps with 40 frames over 33 ms during scrolling. The saved Services and RED pages have only 977 to 1,113 DOM nodes; a "long live page" is not a cost for the actors because #main-content is hidden while the album shows.

### build-wiring

The layer is one built entry (experiences/src/host/index.js built to dist/aixc/aixc.js) that does phases 1-2 only: settings from its own script tag, PageHost regions, the live adapter (read, tag, watch, page map), all under one guard.
