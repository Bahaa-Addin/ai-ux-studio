# ADR-0065: The portal on Vodafone's pages: sticker links only, one fetch and a script-free frame, nothing of Vodafone's copied or written, and no failure that traps a click

- Status: Proposed (2026-11-14). Built in phase 3 step S13 (2026-11-14). Accepted after the owner's live smoke (the real pages and cache headers have not been seen by this code, O29)
- Date: 2026-11-14
- Area: the Pocket's portal transition (`experiences/src/pocket/portal/*`, clone-local ADR-0005), the Pocket chunk's loading (ADR-0052), the layer's guard (ADR-0048 §7), AGENTS.md §4.6; supersedes the live plan's `holdLive` and `portal.css`'s rules on the accessibility button for live pages (ADR-0064)
- Owner decisions: 2026-11-13, OD-3 (the portal on sticker clicks only; Vodafone's own links stay native) and OD-4 (one fetch of the destination, drawn in a script-free frame, then the real navigation served from the HTTP cache, proven by a request-counting test; else a picture preview with no extra request); 2026-11-14, nothing of Vodafone's is lifted (ADR-0064)

## Context

The portal turns a plain click on a link in the album into a transition of about 3.3 s and then navigates. Made for the Services clone, it would on a live page have: caught Vodafone's own header and footer links (73 to 74 on Services) because its listener is on `document`; re-run Vodafone's accessibility widget by copying it into its wall (an Angular element at the body); copied inline `onclick` handlers onto its navigation link, so `footerHandler` ran twice; framed the destination with a second document request (Vodafone's server counts two page views; the frame shows raw server HTML with no lazy pictures and hidden carousels, a visible seam); left `html { overflow: hidden }` and an overlay behind after a guard stop; swallowed every later link click after one failed start; navigated on Escape, which on a Buy-now sticker is not a cancel; and pinned Vodafone's accessibility button inline (`holdLive`) and restyled it (`portal.css`).

## Decision

1. **Sticker links only.** On live the portal listens on the album root and handles only `.pocket-sticker a[href]`. Vodafone's header, footer and cards, the guide's and the layer's own links are never handled and never prevented. Hosts: the page origin only. A plain click (no modifier, a same-tab http(s) link, not a download, not hash-only) is the only thing it takes.
2. **Nothing of Vodafone's is touched.** `holdLive` and `portal.css`'s rules on `#vodafone-aw-icon` and the guide's `--vg-lift` are the clone's alone (ADR-0064: nothing is lifted). The wall never copies Vodafone's accessibility widget (`accessibility-widget`), the cookie banner (`#onetrust-consent-sdk`) or the guide; any element whose tag is a defined custom element is replaced in the copy by an inert stub; root-level frames, video, audio and canvas are stubbed.
3. **One fetch, a script-free frame, then the navigation.** The destination is fetched once (`fetch`, same-origin credentials) and drawn in an iframe from `srcdoc` with an injected `<base href>` and no `allow-scripts`; the frame never requests the document itself, and never runs Vodafone's scripts (its theme calls `localStorage.clear()`, sends page views and renders OneTrust). The real navigation then follows and is expected to be served from the HTTP cache (Vodafone's HTML is `Cache-Control: max-age=14400, public`). A request-counting test proves that on a server that sends those headers; if the cache were not reused, the live default is the picture preview, which makes no extra request. A failed, non-HTML, challenge-page or slow fetch falls back to the preview and the navigation proceeds natively. Raw server HTML will not match the rendered page pixel for pixel: the "last frame equals the destination" check of ADR-0005 is the clone's.
4. **The navigation copies no handlers.** The real navigation is a fresh link with only `href`, `target`, `rel` and `referrerpolicy`, so no inline `on*` attribute of Vodafone's runs twice.
5. **No failure traps a click; no failure stops the layer.** Any error while starting or running a transition ends in the plain navigation of the clicked link, and the portal stays off for the page; the pending state is reset; errors are reported (`[AIXC]`) but do not reach the guard. `destroy()` (also on a guard stop, `?aixc=off` at run time) cancels a running transition and gives the page back: no overlay, no `html[data-vf-portal]`, no scroll lock, no listener. **Escape cancels** on live (the person stays on the Pocket page); on the clone it still navigates, as today.
6. **Hygiene.** Every node the portal adds is marked `data-aixc` and `data-vg`; its stylesheet is text through `ensureCss` (marked); its listeners and timers run through the host's catch-all wrapper.
7. **Loading and Back.** The portal is a chunk of its own, loaded when the album opens; the scene (the 3D hall) is on-demand inside it. A stand-in in the Pocket chunk reports the portal as enabled at once, so the album's sticker links are same-tab links, and a click before the chunk is there is a plain same-tab navigation. The portal marks the history entry (`replaceState` with `vfPortal`, never `pushState`); when a page loads with that mark the layer loads the Pocket and the portal at once (not at idle) and reopens the album at the same scroll, from the bfcache too.

## Consequences

- A sticker opens its page with the transition on live pages; every other link of Vodafone's behaves exactly as without the layer.
- One document request per click instead of two (when the cache holds), no second page view in Vodafone's analytics from the frame's scripts (none run).
- The portal's failure can never leave the person on a dead link.

## Pilot knobs

- Frame (fetch and srcdoc) or picture preview on live; the fetch timeout and the "enough content" test; whether Escape cancels or navigates; the transition's length.

## Revisit when

- The real pages' cache headers differ (the navigation is not served from the cache): the live default becomes the preview.
- Vodafone's pages change their link markup inside stickers' destinations, or put a bot check in front of a page.
- The owner wants the portal on Vodafone's own links as well (OD-3).
