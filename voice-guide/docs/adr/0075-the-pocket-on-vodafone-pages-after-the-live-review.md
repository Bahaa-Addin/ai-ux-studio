## Context

A review panel then looked at the 271 screenshots and found visual defects that the assertions could not see. A usage limit cut the panel off before most findings were cross-checked (`output/live-verify-2026-11-15/review-panel-raw.json`, 105 findings).

On 2026-11-17 the checks were run again, before any fix, so each finding could be confirmed on fresh evidence. Conditions: Google Chrome, the demo profile, public pages, 1.2 s apart, the cookie banner never answered, from a scratch worktree at `a6620cd`, `VG_LIVE_OUT` outside the repository. Evidence is in `output/live-verify-2026-11-17/` (git-ignored): `floating-before/`, `motion-before/`, `confirmation.md`.

- Motion: passed, 12 page loads (cap 16), 17 page requests (12 documents, 4 portal fetches, 1 self-reload of `/en/vodafone-cash`), 24 presses, 228 screenshots. Every motion JPEG was 1000x1590 px, which is the top-left 500x795 CSS px at a device pixel ratio of 2, not the whole viewport.

The fresh evidence confirmed these findings: F1 fonts, F2a and F2b the page turn, F3 the carousels after closing, F4 the portal's scrollbar shift, F5 the wall's copy, F6 the drone, F7a the Arabic Back chevron, F7b the Arabic peel corner, F8a the mic over the last sticker's link, F9 the hero chip under the pill, F10 the band peel slab (at 375 px), F11 a layout on every frame at rest, F12 the journey's preview blank, F13 the pill's name repeated, F14 the Cash picture cropped, F15 the test gaps, NEW-2 the album reopening mid-scroll after the portal's Back without the mascot within 3 s, NEW-7 the curl rule that could never fire, NEW-8 the replay cursor ring half cut.

## Decision

1. **Vodafone's own bold and light faces, on every page; the album's headings in the bold one.** Cause: three of Vodafone's themes declare only `vodafone-regular-en` and `-ar` (`maintheme` on `/en|ar/home`; `static-lite-theme` on `/en|ar/mobile-internet-bundles`, `/en|ar/wireless-net` and `/en/promotions`); only `static-theme` declares all nine names. The chips and the album drew in Helvetica or Geeza Pro there (measured with CDP `CSS.getPlatformFontsForNode`, before the fix). Now `pocket.css` declares four aliases, `aixc-vodafone-{bold,light}-{en,ar}`, from Vodafone's own files at `/theme-assets/static-theme/fonts/splitted-fonts/<lang>/vodafone-<w>-<lang>.woff` (its own origin, root-relative, `font-display: swap`, no weight). A stack is the page's own names first, then the aliases, then the page's regular face. The font variables follow any element's own language (`[lang|=en]`, `[lang|=ar]`), so a sticker kept in the other language draws in that language's faces; `.pocket-sticker__desc` reads the variable. The album's own headings (`h1 .pocket-view__title`, `h2 .pocket-group__title`, `h3` and `h2 .pocket-sticker__title`) draw in `var(--pocket-bold)`, with a selector that outranks the theme's `[dir] h1/h2/h3` rules (those set only `font-family` and won). The portal's preview stacks use the variables. Vodafone's Arabic files are named "GE SS Two" and have no digits, so the `-en` alias draws them. No new font is shipped (AGENTS.md §4.8). Amends ADR-0052 §7 (the fonts are variables with Vodafone's Arabic names first) and §12 (the chip is Vodafone Bold at 0.82 rem).
2. **`#main-content` stays laid out while the album covers it.** `hideSource` in `page-host.js` no longer sets plain `display: none`. It writes a style attribute (`display` `!important`, `height: 0`, `overflow: hidden`, `opacity: 0`, all `!important`), plus `inert` and `hidden`, saves the old attribute and restores it byte for byte (the style is written as the attribute, because Chrome leaves `style=""` after a CSSOM write). It uses `opacity`, not `visibility`: an inherited `visibility` starts Vodafone's `.owl-item` `transition: all` and the cards flash. The kit gains a `fixedHeader` mode `scroll` and an `owlAutoRefresh` stand-in so the offline tests behave like the live page. Amends ADR-0048 §6 (the `PageHost` hides the source; "hidden" now means laid out but not painted).
3. **The turning sheet is cut at Vodafone's header; turn copies carry no live fixed elements; the closing copy keeps liners.** The plane and lift layers are clipped on every turn and peek frame at `seams.headerBottom`, the bottom of `#header` or `#nav-header` when its box touches the top, pinned or not. Turn copies hide (`visibility` and `opacity`) every element that is fixed on the live page (`nav.nav-fixed`, the skip link, the nudge, `nav-step-three`), so the header is no longer doubled. The closing copy keeps the chips region with the liners only (the CTAs are removed), and the chips overlay's `view()` runs `pass()` in the frame the page returns, so the pocketed card is not shown unpocketed and the liner does not pop in. Amends ADR-0052 §5 (what the copy holds and where the turn is cut) and ADR-0064 §3 and §4 (the bottom-left turn).
4. **The portal keeps the page's gutter, paints no strip, and the wall holds no piece that is off screen.**
   - `html[data-vf-portal='active'][data-vf-portal-gutter] { scrollbar-gutter: stable }` is set only when a classic scrollbar takes width (`innerWidth > clientWidth` at the press); `cameraFor` reads `clientWidth` before. The accessibility button and the mic no longer shift sideways for the 3.3 s of the transition.
   - The wall's painted scrollbar strip is removed: a fixed overlay cannot paint into a reserved gutter (measured). Trade-off: during the transition the gutter, 15 px wide on this Mac, shows the page's background, not a scrollbar.
   - `fitFrame` in `handoff.js` gives the drawn destination `overflow: hidden` and `scrollbar-gutter: auto`. In right-to-left an iframe puts its scrollbar on the left; the left column seen live in Arabic was the frame's.
   - `wall.js` `offScreen()` and `hideIn()`: positioned or transformed elements wholly off screen, or clipped to 1 px, get `visibility: hidden` and `opacity: 0` in the copy. Cause: fixed copies are placed from the transformed wall, so `.skip-link { position: fixed; top: -100px }` and the mobile menu (translated off screen) came into view.
   - Amends ADR-0065 §2 (what the wall copies) and answers its "Not verified" line on scrollbars that take space: measured live on a Mac with classic scrollbars, and fixed as above.
5. **Album details.**
   - The Back chevron and the sticker link's external arrow are mirrored when `[dir=rtl]`; `.pocket-back` padding and the journey's "Your journey" padding are logical (F7a).
   - **Pictures are shown whole only when cover would hide too much, on live pages only** (F14). The fit is decided when the picture loads (a seam, `seams.fitPictures`; the clone's album is byte-identical to before). The picture's aspect is P (width over height). It is `contain` (shown whole) only when `cover` would hide more than 40% of it at the frame's measured aspect **and** the picture is tall or square (P < 1.2) or a strip (P > 3.5). Frames measured: 247x150 (1.65) at 1366 px, 329x150 (2.19) at 375 px. Measured: the Cash picture, 218x240, hides 45% at 1366, 59% at 375 and 63% at 414 (frame 368x150), so it is shown whole; a 230x170 photo hides 18%, 38% and 45%, so it stays `cover` at all three; Roaming, 350x150, stays `cover`. The first rule, 40% alone, was too wide: it turned 4:3 photos into insets on 390 to 414 px phones. `pocket-details` F14 failed with that rule at 414 and passes with the narrower one.
   - **The chips of first-screen cards keep clear of the trail's pill and the guide's mic at scroll 0** (F9, F8b, F8a). The overlay reads the pill and `vg-guide`'s mic (and its keyboard button and bar when shown) where they rest at scroll 0, and picks again on `vg:trail-ready`, on a `ResizeObserver` on the pill and the mic, and on a `MutationObserver` on `body`'s direct children for the guide arriving; never on scroll. At 375 px `vodafone-flex` [335,638,367,670] and `end-of-call` [235,631,322,663] were under the mic [296,657,360,721]; now clear. (F8b, "the mic near chips while it listens", stays by design: ADR-0064 places the mic. What is built is the chips clearing the mic where it rests.) The album's foot is 172 px on live pages: the mic's top is 155 px above the screen's bottom at 1366x900 and at 375x812, so the last sticker's link stays clear.
   - Amends ADR-0052 §7 (stickers shown as kept: the picture fit, the foot), §11 (when the corner is picked again) and §12 (the corner order now also avoids the pill and the mic). Test: `tests/e2e/pocket-details.e2e.test.ts` (port 4467), 5 tests (F7a, F14, F9 and F8b, F8a), each failed first.
6. **The actors make room, and are there when the album is.** (F6, F6b, NEW-2.) Amends ADR-0052 part 3, §16 (the drone's place) and §17 (the obstacle list).
   - **The drone has a band and candidates (F6).** Live, the drone was never seen at 1366 (the matrix's `measure.drone` was null in every cell). `measureDrone` now starts the band under the bar below the journey's band and ends it above the first group's head; a new candidate beside the title block (`title-right`, `title-left`) sits on the content margin; the order is band, beside the title, end gutter, far gutter. All of the bar's buttons and the journey band are obstacles, and fixed controls (the pill, the mic, the accessibility button, the banner) count against the home position too. Tests: 4 in `pocket-actors-live.e2e.test.ts` (port 4479; 1366x900 and 1280x800, English and Arabic, trail on): 0 of 4 before (`data-drone-anchor` none, state hidden), 4 of 4 after.
   - **The drone and the mascot make room when a fixed control comes or goes while the album is open (F6b).** Found when the floating rehearsal ran with F6 in: in the state where the trail's consent card opens from the pill over the album, the drone stayed at its home under the card ([1165,233,1256,297] against the card [930,156,1350,560]; the Arabic twin too). `obstacles.js` `watchFixedObstacles(onChange)` holds a `ResizeObserver` per known control (the accessibility button's icon, the banner, the mic, the keyboard button, the bar, the pill, the card) seeded with its current size, a class and `transitionend` watch, and a `MutationObserver` on `body`'s children and on the guide's shadow root. The actors refit at most once per frame. The drone flies to its next free home, or hides while none is free and comes back; the move is eased (1.1 ms per px, 450 to 1,600 ms) because the bare spring overshot by 259 px on a 1,220 px move. The mascot re-measures when a control meets his place: an instant move, as on a resize. Measured at 1366 px: the drone hides 3 ms after the card shows (no free place), is back 1 ms after it closes and at rest after about 106 ms; at 1600 px it flies to the left gutter. Tests: 7 cases in `pocket-actors-live` and 1 unit test; `pocket-actors-live` 26 of 26.
   - **The actors are loaded when the album opens, and after the portal's Back (NEW-2).** After the portal's Back the album reopens at the sticker (ADR-0065 §7) but the mascot was absent for over 3 s, because the actors chunk loaded at idle. The actors now load at once on Back and whenever the album opens; if the album opened before they were ready, its opening is played to them when they are. The ready wait watches the album root's attributes without naming the actors' attribute (so `sdk-size` still finds no actor names in the Pocket chunk). Test: the Back case in `pocket-portal-live`: the mascot is shown 0 ms after the album (before: still null after 1 s).
7. **The peel starts at the card's top-end corner and crosses a long card's height, not its width.** (F7b, F10.) Amends ADR-0052 §12 (which listed the Arabic corner as known) and ADR-0064 (the peel's start and shape).
   - **Arabic (F7b).** On Vodafone's pages the hover lift, the peel (and Put back) and the album's `peelAway` start at the card's top-end corner by its computed direction, which is top-left in Arabic. The clone keeps top-right: its chip is at the physical right in Arabic too. The throw's spin is unchanged: it follows the bottom-left landing corner, so it is not mirrored for a left peel.
   - **A long card (F10).** A card is long when it is wider than 2.5 times its height, or wider than 60% of the screen while at least 1.5 times as wide as tall (the 1.5 keeps near-square phone cards from counting as long). A long card peels across its height: tilt at most 3 degrees, lift at most 6, the sheet held over its slot, the ground shadows fading, shrinking toward 360 px (`STICKER_W`) and never shorter than 48 px. Measured with the kit: the Cash card painted at most 0 and 4 px past its box in the peel and the lift at 1366 px (before: 182 and 200 px, and 1,159 px wide at the throw), and 403 px wide at most from the lift's end; at 375 px 0 and 24 px (before 79 and 173). Tests: `tests/e2e/pocket-peel.e2e.test.ts` (port 4470), 8 tests, 7 of them failed first.
8. **The journey's memory is recorded while the album covers the page, and a blocked node keeps an empty box.** (F12, NEW-8.) Amends ADR-0050's host interface by adding an optional method (trail host API v2, part 4); a host that does not provide it behaves as before.
   - **`setCover({ node, covered(), shown() })` (F12).** The journey's preview of the current page was blank (only the header), because the rrweb snapshot was taken while the album hid the page. The journey chunk registers `#main-content` with `covered` set to `PageHost.sourceHidden`. While covered, the recorder writes any full snapshot with `#main-content`'s shown attributes (in the serialized copy only; the page is not touched) and does not record the host's hide and show of `style`, `inert` and `hidden`. Deferring the first snapshot was the first idea; it would have left no memory while the album is open. Known: if the album opens before the journey chunk registers (for example Back into the album while the trail starts), that first hide is recorded as before.
   - **The grey square (F12) and the cursor ring (NEW-8).** The grey square was Vodafone's accessibility button, blocked by policy and replayed as a default 55x55 button: `sanitize.ts` marks blocked nodes `data-vg-blocked` and `replay.ts` keeps their box empty. The memory frame hides `.replayer-mouse` and its tail (`journey.css`), so the half-cut ring is no longer shown.
   - Tests: `pocket-journey-live` 12 of 12 (3 new: English at 1366, Arabic at 375, the end of the memory); `trail.test.ts` +1 unit test.
9. **The chips are a chunk of their own.** Amends ADR-0052 §15 (loading). The Pocket chunk had grown from 32,155 B to 33,938 B with the fixes above. A new demo entry, `experiences/src/host/demos/chips.js`, holds the overlay and its placement, with `experiences/src/pocket/chips-overlay.css` (the "Pocket it on Vodafone's own pages" section, moved from `pocket.css` verbatim; every selector starts at `.pocket-chips`, which the clone never uses). `host/demos/pocket.js` asks for it first thing in `start()` where chips can show (the website, coverage at least the gate) and when a later reading lifts coverage over the gate. `initPocket` gains `useChips(create)`; the clone's synchronous `createChips` path is kept. Result: Pocket chunk 33,938 to 30,118 B (budget 29.5 KB = 30,208 B, back under the owner's 32,000 B); chips chunk 4,616 B (budget 4.75 KB). Cost: the first chip after the Pocket's start (kit, `/en/services` at 1366, 24 loads) takes a median 12.6 ms against 3.6 ms (max 21.9 against 11.6). The `pocket-chips` test asserts that below the gate there is no chips region at all ("none shows").

## What was held, and why

- **F11, the album at rest forces a layout on every frame (rest mode).** Measured live: LayoutCount +301 in 5 s against 0 for the page alone; script 0.46 to 0.96 s against about 0.012 s. A fix was built in a scratch worktree and measured: LayoutCount 180 to 4 and script 0.25 to 0.04 s per 3 s. At rest the rigs are drawn every 750 ms and the motion between drawings runs as Web Animations keyframes on boxes (an affine fit per sheet), the rotors as an opacity flipbook, the blinks as a lid overlay, the board in a `foreignObject` and the floor in its own `svg`. It costs +3.9 KB gzipped (actors 44,153 to 48,030 B before the drone work; the budget would be 47 KB) and the experiences actor source budget 285 to 310 KB. It also changes how the actors look at rest: the leg step of a weight shift, the glow pulse (every 750 ms), the rotors (15 degree steps) and the blink (a lid). **Held for the owner's look review**, not landed. The patch, rebased on the drone fix (F6), is `output/live-verify-2026-11-17/held-F11-rest-mode-with-F6.patch`; the evidence is in `output/live-verify-2026-11-17/f11-evidence/`. The work also found that the "calm" every-other-frame path never ran, because `animator.clip` was never reset; that finding stayed open with the patch. **Fixed 2026-11-17 in `edc544d`:** the rest mode's port also fixed the "calm" every-other-frame path (the calm check now reads whether a clip's pose tracks are still playing, `clipBusy` from `animator.tick()`, instead of `animator.clip === null`, which never became null).

## The rest mode (F11), landed 2026-11-17 (`edc544d`)

- **What was held.** Rest mode was held for the owner's look review ("What was held, and why", O30). The owner watched the side-by-side recording on 2026-11-17 and approved it, to be ported onto the head and landed. The head had moved: F6b's refit had landed after the patch was built.
- **What landed.** At rest (the mascot idle, dozing or tucked and settled; the pointer and every other input quiet for 1.5 s; the drone hovering at home or with nothing to fly) the physics runs ahead on a saved copy of the state, its random generator included, and the path to the next drawing becomes Web Animations keyframes on boxes only. Both actors are drawn every 750 ms and move on the compositor in between. The mascot's box and sheet, his board, the floor, and the drone's button, svg and shadow each follow a least-squares affine fit to points matched by name. Rotors are a six-page opacity flipbook; blinks are a lid clipped to the eye. Any input that changes what the actors read, any change of either brain's state, a relayout, the fixed-obstacle watch (F6b's refit), reduced motion, a hidden tab or the album's lifecycle ends the rest first: both catch up to now and draw every frame again. `refit()` ends the rest before it moves or hides the drone, and no rest starts during its eased flight.
