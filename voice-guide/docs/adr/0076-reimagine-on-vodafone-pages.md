# ADR-0076: Reimagine on Vodafone's pages: a box in the page, a safe area for the controls, templates only, and the experiences' own colours

- Status: Proposed (2026-11-17): phase 4a is built and committed (groundwork `3992aa5`, stage 1 `2b7ac42`, stage 2, the shell, `cfa3192`); Accepted after phase 4c's live run and the owner's look
- Date: 2026-11-17
- Area: Reimagine on live pages (`experiences/src/host/demos/reimagine.js` and `reimagine/*`, the launcher chunk, the templates-only composer, `shared.js` and its scroller seam, `experience-wrap.js`, `template-schema.js`, the thirteen experiences), the live adapter's mark scope, the Pocket's resting curl on live pages, the live checks; AGENTS.md §4.6 and §4.8. `aixc.css` is not loaded on live pages.
- Owner decisions: 2026-11-17, see "Owner decisions" below. Design: `docs/reference/live-way2-phase4-design-2026-11-17.md` (§0 holds the answers).
- Numbering: ADR-0068 to ADR-0074 belong to `feature/vg-revamp`, and so does ADR-0077 (the revamp's); ADR-0075 is the live review's fixes. This ADR is 0076 (the owner and the revamp session confirmed it free on 2026-11-17). The next free number is 0078.
- Depends on: ADR-0075 (§2 the hidden source, §3 the header's bottom, §8 the trail's cover, §1 the fonts). If ADR-0075 is not accepted, §1, §6 and §8 below need a second look.

## Context

The plan (`docs/handoff/live-demos-way2.md` §3.3, §3.5 row 3, phase 4) wants Reimagine on Vodafone's own pages: the composer reads the live model, the page is hidden, the layer's view shows, "Original" brings the page back exactly. Four facts stop us from copying the clone's design.

1. **Sticky does not pin on Vodafone's themes.** The static and static-lite themes set `overflow-x: hidden` on `html` and `body`, so `position: sticky` never sticks. Ten experiences pin a stage that way (fan, coverflow, gallery, cube, spotlight, ring, tunnel, journey, split, streaming), and the view bar is sticky too. A full-screen sheet fixed over the page would fix this, but it covers Vodafone's footer and needs the page's scroll stopped (ADR-0046 §2, ADR-0064 §1, plan §3.3).
2. **The bottom corners are taken.** Bottom-right holds Vodafone's accessibility button and the guide's mic (desktop 1272..1336 x 743..880; phone 280..344 x 655..792), plus the guide's bar and keyboard button while it listens. Bottom-left holds the Pocket's button (22..80 x 820..878 on desktop; 16..68 x 744..796 on phones) and its resting curl (desktop 0..204 x 639..900; phone 0..166 x 599..812). On a first visit the cookie banner is in one of them too (bottom-left in English, bottom-right in Arabic). ADR-0044's lift of the accessibility button is forbidden on live pages (ADR-0064 §5; AGENTS.md §4.6). The experiences' next, previous and pause controls, spotlight's queue, ring's footer, tunnel's HUD, gallery's caption and other panels sit in those corners today.
3. **Templates only.** The owner decided on 2026-11-10: no Gemini, no key field, no Google Fonts on live pages. The clone's composer carries all three.
4. **The core has 65 B left,** and it imports `content-index.js`, which the composer also needs. Adding the composer as a dynamic demo made esbuild move `content-index.js` into a shared chunk the core imports (+856 B measured earlier).

The map of the code also found smaller gaps (the model changes after load but the composer indexes once; pages without a hero have a null title; two experiences open modal dialogs; the adapter's `tag()` clears our marks; the icon sprite is missing on live pages). They are handled in §5 to §8.

## Decision

1. **The view is a box in the page, as tall as the screen below Vodafone's header, with its own scroll.** While a view shows, `#main-content` is hidden as ADR-0075 §2 describes (laid out, no height, transparent, inert). `#experience-output` becomes an in-flow box (`.aixc-box`) with `overflow-y: auto` and `overscroll-behavior: contain`, as the design said; the rule is unchanged. Vodafone's header above, its footer below and the page's own scroll and scrollbar stay as they are. At the view's end a closing band and "Original page" lead back; scrolling the page past the box reaches Vodafone's footer. Inside the box `position: sticky` works, so the ten pinning experiences keep their design.
   - **Built in stage 1 (`2b7ac42`): the scroller seam.** `experiences/src/aixc/experiences/shared.js` has `configure({ scroller, wrap, copy })`. Every scroll read and write, listener, frame and timer of the shared helpers goes through the scrolling element of our own; with nothing configured the scroller is the window, and the clone's numbers are unchanged over a 252-case grid. Magazine and spotlight add their listeners on mount and remove them on unmount and expose `reveal(itemId)`. The other experiences' own `window` listeners move in 4b. `--vx-top` is split into a pin top (the box's coordinates) and a viewport top (anything fixed) in the same work.
   - **Stage 1 also built the box's rules for magazine and spotlight on the clone side:** `experiences/tests/e2e/box_e2e.py` runs both in a scrolling box, eleven sizes (desktop, laptop, tablet and phone), English and Arabic: 1,228 checks, of which 464 fail without the box rules.
   - **Stage 2 (the shell, landed in `cfa3192`, 2026-11-17):** the box itself on live pages: `#experience-output.aixc-box` with `#aixc-viewbar` as its sticky first row, a stage, and an end band ("Original page", "Continue to the page"). Resting rule: on open the page scrolls so the box sits under the header (up to four corrections). Where it cannot, because the page cannot scroll far enough (on the saved pages only 51 px are above the box once the nav pins), the box rests as close as it can with its bottom at the screen's bottom (its height is the window's inner height minus its top). An always-pinned nav sets `--aixc-box-push`. A resize re-settles the box when the window has not scrolled since. Views keep the window's scroll (B-28) and the box's scroll.
   - Nothing of Vodafone's is covered, moved or locked. No `overflow` is set on `html` or `body`, so the page's scrollbar, and with it Vodafone's accessibility button, never shift (ADR-0075 §4).
   - The clone's suites guard the shared-code change. After stage 1: `aixc_e2e` 298/298, `arabic_e2e` 71/71, `pocket_e2e` 210/210, `tips_e2e` 87/87, `portal_e2e` 54/56 (AIXC-95's two frame checks, as before).

2. **A safe area for the experiences' controls, and the Pocket's resting curl hidden during a view.** On live pages there are two physical safe zones, the same in English and Arabic: bottom-right (Vodafone's accessibility button, the guide's mic, and the guide's bar and keyboard button while it listens) and bottom-left (the Pocket's button). Every pressable element of an experience stays out of both zones. The zones are measured raw from those controls' boxes by the shell and set as custom properties on our box, never on Vodafone's `<html>`; the experiences' own CSS maps them to the physical sides, so each side keeps clear of its own zone.
   - **Built in stage 1, for spotlight and magazine only (in a box of the clone's harness, `box_e2e.py`).** In `.aixc-box` nothing pressable sits in either physical bottom zone. Spotlight's controls are at the bottom centre, placed from each zone and lifted above the taller zone when the stage is narrow; on phones its row is under the slide's title. Spotlight's queue shows only from 1180 px wide and 850 px tall; it is hidden on narrower windows (phones and 720 to 1179 px) and on shorter ones (the flowing layout). From 1180 px the stage is the room's height and the bottom reserve is the zones' height; windows under 850 px tall use a flowing layout. At 1024x768 and 768x1024 the stage equals the room. The experiences' fonts read the shared faces' variables.
   - The other eleven experiences (fan, ring, tunnel, gallery, coverflow, split, cube, canvas, flipbook, streaming, journey) are re-laid out one by one in 4b (ring's footer and coverflow's phone panel need a small redesign).
   - **Known gaps in what is built:** (1) on windows under 700 px tall some stages exceed the room by up to 25 px; (2) with the guide listening at 1440x900 and 1600x850 the Arabic CTA of two cards reaches 5 to 8 px into the 472 px right zone; (3) the 850 px switch to the flowing layout was measured on the Services copy only; (4) stage 2 adds one more: at `/ar/services` at 1024x768, with the guide listening and before the stage pins, spotlight's CTA is 17 px inside the right zone (the one `[gap]` in the stage-2 tests). All four are recorded in O38 and O39 and are measured, not fixed.
   - **Accessibility, skipped per the owner's 2026-11-14 decision ("skip accessibility issues fixes for now"):** on phones spotlight's controls row shows under the title but comes after the CTA in tab order (visual order is not tab order); and with a view open the page has two level-1 headings (Vodafone's screen-reader-only header heading and our `h1`). They are O37 and O40 in `docs/qa/bugs-and-open-issues.md`.
   - While a view shows, the Pocket's resting curl hides (opacity 0). Its button stays, keeps its count, and opens the album with the page turn. This brings back ADR-0044's tuck for live pages without the lift. **It amends ADR-0064 §5**, which made the tuck clone-only. The rule lives in Reimagine's CSS (`live.css`: `body[data-aixc-view='reimagined']:not([data-pocket-view])` on the curl's layers; the Pocket chunk had 10 B left at `2b7ac42`, 30,198 of 30,208 B). **Built in stage 2** (`cfa3192`): the e2e checks the curl hidden in a view and shown during the album's turn.
   - A test checks that every pressable element of each experience is clear of both zones at 1366 and 375, in English and Arabic. Stage 1's `box_e2e.py` does this for magazine and spotlight; stage 2's e2e re-checks spotlight in the real box at 1366x900, 1366x768, 1024x768 and 375x812, English and Arabic (the stage pinned at the bar with its bottom at the box's, nothing pressable in a zone, the row under the title on phones); magazine's zones are checked only in `box_e2e.py` so far (its real-box case checks the box, the items and the h1). The rest come with 4b.

3. **Launcher, composer and view bar.**
   - **The launcher (built in stage 1, `2b7ac42`; `experiences/src/host/demos/reimagine.js`, loaded by the core at idle).** Gate: website pages whose reading has coverage 0.9 or more and two sections of three or more items, re-checked on every reading (§4). Place: directly below the trail's pill on the pill's side, measured from the pill's box so it follows the trail (B-23), and **tucking with the pill** (a `MutationObserver` on the pill's class sets `data-tucked`; no layout read per frame; keyboard focus on the launcher brings it back). With no pill it sits under the header's bottom plus 16 px and is shown only while `scrollY` is under 120 (the trail's own "near the top"), tucked otherwise: a launcher left in place covered Vodafone's cards and chips after a scroll (lead decision, 2026-11-17). Hidden while the trail's consent card, sheet, toast or journey shows, while the album shows, while a view shows, while any of Vodafone's menus or dialogs is open (by CSS `:has()` on `visibility`) and while Vodafone's sign-in card for signed-out visitors shows. z-index 19: above Vodafone's in-flow content, below its pinned nav, far below OneTrust. It comes in the DOM before `#aixc-viewbar`. `.aixc-launcher` and its `aria-expanded` stay (the floating matrix and Tips read them, ADR-0045 §6). It imports nothing the core or the Pocket imports. The chips keep clear of it on the first screen, keep its resting place while it hides, and re-check at the top. A press loads the shell. Chunk 3,151 B gzipped (budget 3.125 KB = 3,200 B).
   - **Stage 2 (the shell, `cfa3192`):** the composer is a new small module with the template buttons (magazine and spotlight in 4a; the 13 in 4b) and an Original / Reimagined segment, not the clone's `initComposer` with flags. It has no `gemini-client.js`, settings, key field, prompt box or cache. A test asserts the shell's and the launcher's chunks are free of `generativelanguage`, `aixc-key`, `aixc-model`, `fonts.googleapis`, `x-goog-api-key` and `codepen`, and that the core has no `aixc-composer`. The clone's Gemini and key copy used to reach the shell through `i18n.js`; 51 entries moved to `aixc/composer-words.js`, and 'Gemini', 'API key' and 'مفتاح' are forbidden in Reimagine's chunks (the `i18n` chunk went from 8,314 to 6,171 B). It keeps the class names, ids and `body[data-aixc-view]` that the Pocket and Tips read. A press on the launcher during a load sets `aria-busy` and further presses are ignored (B-78). The composer is non-modal, closes on Escape and with its close button, and is closed by Vodafone's menus and dialogs and by the album. A template still loading is dropped when the reader moves on (Original, Reset, Escape, the album, another template) and kept when a Vodafone menu closes the composer (B-77, B-83). The Pocket gets a composer handle: the album unmounts and remounts the view, and its page turn copies the box's scroll. The clone keeps its own composer until phase 9.
   - **The composer's placements (stage 2, B-82).** BELOW (under the header, its opener and the pill, +8 px) when the room down to the corner controls is at least `H_MIN` 216 px (measured: the whole composer in English; in Arabic 235 px below 320 px wide). Otherwise BESIDE: a column on the inline-start side of the opener (left in English, right in Arabic), from the header +8 px to the screen's bottom -16 px, at most 400 px wide and at least `W_MIN` 240 px (measured), cut clear of the corner zones (16 px) and of the pill, the launcher, the bar's controls and the view's name (8 px). Otherwise BELOW, scrolling in the room that is left. It never covers its opener, the pill, the launcher, a corner control or Vodafone's header. Tested beside its opener at 812x375, 667x375 and 844x390 (phones in landscape), 1366x380 and 1366x450, English and Arabic, from the launcher and from "Change experience"; below at 375x360 (both openers) and 375x420 (launcher), English (at 667x375 in English from "Change": a 278x249 column holding a 278x216 composer).
   - The view bar is the first row inside the box and is sticky there. Its inline end stays clear of the trail's pill (the pill's measured box plus 16 px of padding); on phones the bar takes its own row below the pill. **Stage 2** names the view bar and the composer in the matrix's hit naming (`viewbar`, `composer`); making them matrix controls that rules (a) and (c) check is B2. B-23 for the bar is checked by `reimagine-live.e2e` ("the view bar's buttons are never under the trail's pill").

4. **The gate: every CMS page that passes, checked on each page load.** A page can be reimagined when the model's coverage is at least 0.9 and it has at least two sections of three or more items. The check runs on each page load, so a page that reads badly has no launcher rather than a broken view. This answers design question D3. Measured on the saved pages of 2026-10-23 through the live adapter (design §6): `/home`, `/services`, `/mobile-internet-bundles` and `/vodafone-red1` pass in both languages; `/vodafone-cash`, `/promotions` and `/wireless-net` do not. Coverage has never been measured on the live pages (O29). Built in stage 1 in the launcher chunk; `reimagine-launcher.e2e` (9 cases, each failing first) checks it on the saved pages through the live adapter, English and Arabic, at 1366x900 and 375x812.

5. **Loading on intent.** Amends the loading rule of ADR-0046 §1 only by naming the pieces.
   - **A launcher chunk** (the launcher, the gate, the place and hide rules) loads at idle where the layer runs; built (3,151 B at 2b7ac42, 3,191 B at cfa3192). `AIXC_LOADED_BY` is core, then launcher, then shell.
   - **The shell chunk** (composer, view bar, validator, i18n) loads when the launcher is pressed. Stage 1 left a stub (`reimagine/shell.js`, `open(ctx)`). **Stage 2:** the shell, 23,704 B gzipped (budget 25,088 B; 6,171 B of it is `i18n.js`, which the Pocket has already loaded). `aixc.css` is not part of it and is not loaded on live pages; the experiences carry their own CSS.
   - **One chunk per experience**, loaded when its template is chosen, from a live-only registry (`src/host/demos/reimagine/`). The clone's synchronous `experience-renderer.js` is left alone. `experience-wrap.js` (the mount wrapper, `wrapExperience(output)` returning `{ element, mounted, mount(), unmount(), reveal(itemId) }`) is shared with the clone's renderer; `template-schema.js` holds the template schemas without pen data (`buildGroupSchema`, `buildTemplateSchema`, `TEMPLATE_CARDS`), and the live composer uses these, never `templates.js`. Stage 2: magazine 10,505 B, spotlight 7,731 B.
   - **`content-index` stays out of the core** (built, `3992aa5`). `normalizeContentModel` and its constants moved to `experiences/src/host/model.js`, which imports nothing; the clone's adapter imports it from there too (a re-export from `content-index.js` would have made `model.js` a shared chunk of the core: 8,265 B against 8,027 B with a probe). With a demo chunk importing `content-index.js` the core measured 8,852 B before the move and 8,027 B after. `experiences/tests/core-graph.test.mjs`, and a probe build in `sdk-size.test.ts` (`build.ts` exports `aixcBuildOptions`; the chunks it builds are identical, checked by hashing the built file list), each fail on the old graph.
   - **The seam** (stage 1): `configure()` is the only door between the shared helpers and the page's scroller (§1, §6).
   - Budgets are set after the first build, a little above what is measured, each with a dated comment. The Pocket's budget is watched too, because new shared chunks can move files out of its closure (§8).

6. **The guard, the model, the guide, the trail.**
   - **Guard (the seam owns its listeners).** The first draft gave renderers a `listen()` built on `guard.listen`. Stage 1 found that `guard.listen` keeps a clean-up per call for the guard's whole life, so a listener added and removed many times leaked a clean-up each time. As built, the seam's `on()` wraps each handler with the guard's `wrap`, adds and removes the listener itself, and keeps its own `off`; `configure()` releases them all, and a `listen` passed to `configure()` is ignored. The shell calls `configure({ scroller, wrap: guard.wrap, copy })` and **must call `configure()` from its destroy on `guard.onStop`**: that is what removes the seam's listeners when the layer fails. Order: configure, render, mount; Original: unmount, then `configure()`; Reimagined: `configure({...})`, then mount; destroy: unmount, then `configure()`. The seam also exposes `keptCount()`, `guarded(name, fn)`, `observeResize`, `viewScroller()`, `onViewScroll`, `onViewResize` and `storeLink`. Test: the `shared-seam` unit test "the guard keeps nothing for the seam: its cleanups do not grow across mount, unmount, mount and drags" (it failed on the `guard.listen` version), and `reimagine-live.e2e`'s case "a forced failure ... and the guard's stop over a view" (leftovers: no listener, observer or frame). **Stage 2:** one destroy on `guard.onStop` unmounts the experience, calls `configure()`, removes the composer, live region, view note and sprite, and calls `host.showSource()`; the launcher removes itself on the same guard stop; everything we add carries `data-aixc` and `data-vg`; a forced failure in the view gives the page back as served (`#main-content`'s style, inert and hidden as served, the page's geometry as with `?aixc=off`, nothing of ours left listening).
   - **Model.** Stage 1: `words(key, values)` gives neutral copy ("items") on pages that are not about services, English and Arabic. **Stage 2:** the model is re-indexed from a copy on every `aixc:model`, with the page's name as the title where there is no hero (no empty `h1`); an open view is not re-rendered under the reader, and a view note says when a reading changes the items; the word "services" is used only on `/(en|ar)/services`.
   - **Guide.** Built in the groundwork (`3992aa5`): the live adapter's `tag()` and `untag()` clear marks under `#main-content` only. A re-read used to strip `data-aixc-item` and `data-aixc-section` from every element of the document, so a view's cards would have lost theirs on the first re-read. **This amends ADR-0049 §7.** The selector is `#main-content [data-aixc-item],#main-content [data-aixc-section]` (+4 B: core 8,003 of 8,064 B at the time); `aixc-layer.e2e`'s two new cases failed on the old code (the view's four marks wiped; only the `pocket` sheet) and pass. Stage 1: magazine and spotlight expose `reveal(itemId)` through `wrapExperience`, so the layer can step the stage to a card before the guide spotlights it; the other experiences get theirs in 4b. **Stage 2:** destinations are on one element per section (`data-vg-destination` from `section.destinationId`, ADR-0049 §4), and icons that Vodafone's page lacks are injected as `aixc-` symbols (a store with no icon is a text link, built in stage 1). O19 (Cash routing) stays recorded, not hidden.
   - **Vodafone's dialogs.** A card whose action opens one of Vodafone's own dialogs cannot open it while `#main-content` is inert. It is drawn as a non-link in the view, with no invented `href` (test). Not built yet (4b).
   - **Trail.** The trail does not record the reimagined view (it is ours, `data-vg`). **Stage 2:** the journey and the shell share one trail cover (`host.trailCover`, ADR-0075 §8), so a journey's memory shows the page as served even where the journey chunk has not loaded.

7. **Dialogs on live pages are not modal.** Canvas's and streaming's previews open without the browser's top layer: `dialog.show()` or a `role="dialog"` element inside the box, below Vodafone's accessibility button and the guide, with their own focus trap and Escape. A modal `showModal()` would draw above the accessibility button and the cookie banner and make the rest unusable, which breaks AGENTS.md §4.6. A floating-matrix cell opens each dialog and checks that a press at Vodafone's accessibility button reaches it and that the page's box equals `?aixc=off`'s. Not part of 4a (canvas and streaming come in 4b).

8. **Fonts through the shared `vodafone-faces` stylesheet.** The four `aixc-vodafone-*` aliases and the shared stacks (ADR-0075 §1) move into one stylesheet that the Pocket and Reimagine load by name with `ensureCss('vodafone-faces')`. Built in the groundwork (`3992aa5`): `experiences/src/host/vodafone-faces.css` holds what ADR-0075 §1 put in `pocket.css`, verbatim; the Pocket's chunk loads it as `vodafone-faces` before `pocket`, and the clone's two pages link it before `pocket.css`. Stage 1: the experiences' fonts read the shared faces' variables, so `aixc.css`, which is not loaded on live pages, plays no part. Once Reimagine imports the sheet it becomes a shared chunk of about 364 B gzipped; measured with a probe it put the Pocket 76 B over its budget, and stage 1's six new i18n keys (five, then "Jump to a section" in the review) put the Pocket at 30,198 of 30,208 B at `2b7ac42`. In stage 2 (`cfa3192`) the Pocket is 28,249 B of a budget of 30,464 B (the `i18n` chunk shrank, see §3). The streaming wordmark draws in Vodafone's bold face (4b). An offline font test of the reimagined view runs on the saved `/en/home` and bundles pages (the themes that lack the bold and light faces). No new font is shipped (AGENTS.md §4.8). Known gap: magazine loads `vodafone.css`, which it does not use (2.3 KB).

## Owner decisions

All 2026-11-17. Each is listed with what it was chosen over.

- **The view's shape:** a box in the page with its own scroll. Over: a full-screen sheet fixed under the header (it covers Vodafone's footer and stops the page's scroll).
- **The experiences' controls:** the safe area. Over: shipping first only the experiences that fit (magazine, flipbook, canvas).
- **Colours:** the experiences keep their own palettes, recorded as an exception. Over: mapping them to Vodafone's tokens.
- **Pages (D3):** every CMS page that passes the gate, checked on each page load. Over: a fixed demo list only.
- **Go-ahead:** build phase 4a. ADR number 0076 confirmed.
- **Credits:** the licence text and copyright as a comment at the top of each adapted source file; no visible credit. Over: a visible credit on the page or in the view (the design's three options).
- **Arabic microcopy:** its native review blocks 4c's final live check.
- **Rest mode (F11):** approved after the side-by-side recording, ported onto the head and landed on 2026-11-17 in `edc544d` (see ADR-0075, "The rest mode (F11), landed 2026-11-17"). It is ADR-0075's held item, not part of Reimagine; it is listed here because the owner answered it in the same message.
- **Pocket button's place:** the owner recalled it at the bottom-left. That is ADR-0064, already built and measured live; the safe area's bottom-left zone is that button.

**Lead's design choices in stage 1 (2026-11-17; not the owner's decisions):** the launcher tucks with the pill, and with no pill it shows only while `scrollY` is under 120; spotlight's queue shows in the box only from 1180 px wide and 850 px tall; the chips keep a hidden launcher's scroll-0 rest and re-check at the top; the launcher's z-index is 19 and it comes in the DOM before `#aixc-viewbar`.

## Consequences

- Ten experiences keep their sticky stages on Vodafone's themes, at the price of two scrolls (the page's, then the view's). On phones this may feel unusual.
- `shared.js` changed for the scroller seam (built, stage 1) and every experience's own `window` listeners still have to move (4b). The clone runs the same code, so its suites must pass again after each change; they did after stage 1 (§1).

## Rejected options

- **A full-screen sheet fixed under the header.** It covers Vodafone's footer and needs the page's scroll stopped, which reverses ADR-0046 §2, ADR-0064 §1 and plan §3.3, and risks the 15 px shift that ADR-0075 §4 fixed.
- **Re-implementing pinning per experience with `position: fixed`.** Ten experiences, each fragile; the scroller seam does it once.
- **Shipping only the experiences that do not pin** (magazine, flipbook, canvas). Too thin for a demo.
- **Gemini on live pages.** Against the owner's decision of 2026-11-10. Templates only.
- **Lifting Vodafone's accessibility button and the guide's mic for the view** (ADR-0044's way). Forbidden on live pages (AGENTS.md §4.6, ADR-0064 §1).
- **Mapping the experiences' colours to Vodafone's tokens.** Not chosen by the owner; kept as the way back (see Revisit when).
- **A fixed demo list of pages.** Not chosen; the gate decides (§4).
