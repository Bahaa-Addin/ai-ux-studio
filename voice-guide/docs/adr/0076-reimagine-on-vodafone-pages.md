## Context

The plan (`docs/handoff/live-demos-way2.md` §3.3, §3.5 row 3, phase 4) wants Reimagine on Vodafone's own pages: the composer reads the live model, the page is hidden, the layer's view shows, "Original" brings the page back exactly. Four facts stop us from copying the clone's design.

1. **Sticky does not pin on Vodafone's themes.** The static and static-lite themes set `overflow-x: hidden` on `html` and `body`, so `position: sticky` never sticks. Ten experiences pin a stage that way (fan, coverflow, gallery, cube, spotlight, ring, tunnel, journey, split, streaming), and the view bar is sticky too. A full-screen sheet fixed over the page would fix this, but it covers Vodafone's footer and needs the page's scroll stopped (ADR-0046 §2, ADR-0064 §1, plan §3.3).
2. **The bottom corners are taken.** Bottom-right holds Vodafone's accessibility button and the guide's mic (desktop 1272..1336 x 743..880; phone 280..344 x 655..792), plus the guide's bar and keyboard button while it listens. Bottom-left holds the Pocket's button (22..80 x 820..878 on desktop; 16..68 x 744..796 on phones) and its resting curl (desktop 0..204 x 639..900; phone 0..166 x 599..812). On a first visit the cookie banner is in one of them too (bottom-left in English, bottom-right in Arabic). ADR-0044's lift of the accessibility button is forbidden on live pages (ADR-0064 §5; AGENTS.md §4.6). The experiences' next, previous and pause controls, spotlight's queue, ring's footer, tunnel's HUD, gallery's caption and other panels sit in those corners today.
3. **Templates only.** The owner decided on 2026-11-10: no Gemini, no key field, no Google Fonts on live pages. The clone's composer carries all three.
4. **The core has 65 B left,** and it imports `content-index.js`, which the composer also needs. Adding the composer as a dynamic demo made esbuild move `content-index.js` into a shared chunk the core imports (+856 B measured earlier).

The map of the code also found smaller gaps (the model changes after load but the composer indexes once; pages without a hero have a null title; two experiences open modal dialogs; the adapter's `tag()` clears our marks; the icon sprite is missing on live pages). They are handled in §5 to §8.

## Decision

1. **The view is a box in the page, as tall as the screen below Vodafone's header, with its own scroll.** While a view shows, `#main-content` is hidden as ADR-0075 §2 describes (laid out, no height, transparent, inert). `#experience-output` becomes an in-flow box (`.aixc-box`) with `overflow-y: auto` and `overscroll-behavior: contain`, as the design said; the rule is unchanged. Vodafone's header above, its footer below and the page's own scroll and scrollbar stay as they are. At the view's end a closing band and "Original page" lead back; scrolling the page past the box reaches Vodafone's footer. Inside the box `position: sticky` works, so the ten pinning experiences keep their design.
   - **Built in stage 1 (`2b7ac42`): the scroller seam.** `experiences/src/aixc/experiences/shared.js` has `configure({ scroller, wrap, copy })`. Every scroll read and write, listener, frame and timer of the shared helpers goes through the scrolling element of our own; with nothing configured the scroller is the window, and the clone's numbers are unchanged over a 252-case grid. Magazine and spotlight add their listeners on mount and remove them on unmount and expose `reveal(itemId)`. The other experiences' own `window` listeners move in 4b. `--vx-top` is split into a pin top (the box's coordinates) and a viewport top (anything fixed) in the same work.
   - **Stage 1 also built the box's rules for magazine and spotlight on the clone side:** `experiences/tests/e2e/box_e2e.py` runs both in a scrolling box, eleven sizes (desktop, laptop, tablet and phone), English and Arabic: 1,228 checks, of which 464 fail without the box rules.
   - **Stage 2 (the shell, landed in `cfa3192`, 2026-11-17):** the box itself on live pages: `#experience-output.aixc-box` with `#aixc-viewbar` as its sticky first row, a stage, and an end band ("Original page", "Continue to the page"). Resting rule: on open the page scrolls so the box sits under the header (up to four corrections). Where it cannot, because the page cannot scroll far enough (on the saved pages only 51 px are above the box once the nav pins), the box rests as close as it can with its bottom at the screen's bottom (its height is the window's inner height minus its top). An always-pinned nav sets `--aixc-box-push`. A resize re-settles the box when the window has not scrolled since. Views keep the window's scroll (B-28) and the box's scroll.
   - **Phase 4b (2026-11-18 to 2026-11-21): all thirteen experiences on the seam.** Each of the other eleven reads and writes its scroll only through the seam (no window scroll API), adds its listeners through `on()` on mount and loses them on unmount, runs its frames and timers through `frame()` and `later()`, has `reveal(itemId)` (false for an unknown id), takes its words from `words()` and `countWords()`, and has its box rules under `.aixc-box .aixc-vx--<name>`, a live wrapper (`src/host/demos/reimagine/<name>.js`), a registry line and a budget. Four additions to `shared.js`:
     - `onViewZones(callback)`: a `MutationObserver` on the box's `style`, where the shell writes the corners' zones. An experience re-fences when the Pocket's button shows or the guide listens, even when the box keeps its size. It runs through the guard and the seam keeps it.
     - `countWords(n)`: the clone's `countServices(n)` where the copy is about services; otherwise "1 item" / "12 items", and in Arabic عنصر واحد, عنصرين, 3 to 10 عناصر, 11 and up عنصر (zero takes the singular, as `countServices` does).
     - `keep(undo)`, now exported: the seam keeps `undo` until it runs, and `configure()` runs it if nothing has. `view-dialog.js` keeps an open preview this way (§7).
     - `NEUTRAL_WORDS`, exported and frozen: the eleven's labels in neutral words, which follow the composer's card names where those are neutral ('Card fan', 'Gallery wall', 'Flipbook', 'Ring', 'Cover flow', 'Split slideshow', 'Hyper tunnel', 'Infinite canvas'), the canvas line and the canvas sign 'Vodafone Egypt · {page}'.
   - `pageHeading` gives no `h1` for a blank title; the shell then takes the document's title (`model.title?.trim() || titleOf(document)`), and a fresh view with no heading focuses the bar's "Reimagined".
   - The clone runs the same code and is unchanged: `aixc_e2e` 298/298 and `arabic_e2e` 71/71 after every step. One difference, accepted by the lead as benign: canvas's listeners on the clone are now added on mount and removed on unmount through `on()` (they were added once, at build). The clone behaves the same from mount on.
   - Nothing of Vodafone's is covered, moved or locked. No `overflow` is set on `html` or `body`, so the page's scrollbar, and with it Vodafone's accessibility button, never shift (ADR-0075 §4).
   - The clone's suites guard the shared-code change. After stage 1: `aixc_e2e` 298/298, `arabic_e2e` 71/71, `pocket_e2e` 210/210, `tips_e2e` 87/87, `portal_e2e` 54/56 (AIXC-95's two frame checks, as before).

2. **A safe area for the experiences' controls, and the Pocket's resting curl hidden during a view.** On live pages there are two physical safe zones, the same in English and Arabic: bottom-right (Vodafone's accessibility button, the guide's mic, and the guide's bar and keyboard button while it listens) and bottom-left (the Pocket's button). Every pressable element of an experience stays out of both zones. The zones are measured raw from those controls' boxes by the shell and set as custom properties on our box, never on Vodafone's `<html>`; the experiences' own CSS maps them to the physical sides, so each side keeps clear of its own zone.
   - **Built in stage 1, for spotlight and magazine only (in a box of the clone's harness, `box_e2e.py`).** In `.aixc-box` nothing pressable sits in either physical bottom zone. Spotlight's controls are at the bottom centre, placed from each zone and lifted above the taller zone when the stage is narrow; on phones its row is under the slide's title. Spotlight's queue shows only from 1180 px wide and 850 px tall; it is hidden on narrower windows (phones and 720 to 1179 px) and on shorter ones (the flowing layout). From 1180 px the stage is the room's height and the bottom reserve is the zones' height; windows under 850 px tall use a flowing layout. At 1024x768 and 768x1024 the stage equals the room. The experiences' fonts read the shared faces' variables.
   - **Built in phase 4b for the other eleven**, checked in the box harness (`box_e2e.py`, a row per experience at the sizes it names, at rest and with the guide listening) and in the real box (`reimagine-live.e2e`, a row of `EXPERIENCE_CASES` per experience). The rule for what meets a zone: an item in a corner's zone, or cut by the box's sides, is inert while it is there (fan's `keepClear()`, cover flow, and the `fence()` of ring, gallery, cube and canvas). Journey's, flipbook's and streaming's items under a zone take no pointer events instead (a class, such as streaming's zone cover), so Tab still reaches them, and a focused one is scrolled clear. Tunnel is mixed: only what flies past the stage's sides or into a zone (its slots) is inert; its flowing links in a zone, and its services when laid flat, get `.tn-fenced` (no pointer events) and stay in the Tab order, as journey's, flipbook's and streaming's do. Every item is still reached by the experience's own controls, keys, drag or scroll. The Phase 5 review found where the inert rule lost focus or Tab (B-96). As fixed in `6e017a9`: a focused item that turns inert hands the focus on first (to Next, else Previous, else the stage with `tabindex -1`: ring, split, fan and gallery); and streaming's row cards and bar links are inert only where what their own container shows (the row's viewport, else the bar's list) is past the box's side. A container that scrolls sideways never cuts, so the bar's links are never cut, off-page cards stay focusable, and the row's Tab paging works. Each experience's layout in the box:

In a short box from 720 px wide (phones in landscape) the controls' row moves in past the end corner's zone once that zone reaches it, and in a window under 500 px tall and at least 720 px wide (a media query on the window, not the box) the hint and the progress bar give way, as on phones (B-97, `6e017a9`).

     - **split:** its row and rail out of both zones; the rail is not offered in a short box, and is hidden where it would reach the taller zone (1366x700 with the guide listening: `fitRail()`, `.ss-rail.is-tight`, `6e017a9`), a focused rail button handing the focus on first; on phones the row is under the shown slide's title.
     - **ring:** the foot one column at the bottom centre (beside the ring in a short box); tiers for short phones.
     - **gallery:** its prev / next row and the shown piece's caption out of both zones (bottom centre from 720 px, under the stage's title on phones).
     - **journey:** the scene pins at the bar while the story scrolls over it; the story's links take no pointer events under a corner and are never inert; the closing band above the taller zone at the box's end.
     - **cube:** the stage beside the story from 900 px, under the sheets on phones; its dots a column at the stage's start edge above that side's zone, or a row where the column does not fit (`placeDots()`).
     - **tunnel:** the stage pinned at the bar, the HUD's prev / next out of both zones (lifted above the taller one where the row does not fit); laid flat under reduced motion and in a short box, Tab reaching every service (a link in a zone is `.tn-fenced`, not inert, B-92).
     - **flipbook:** no pinning. The controls row at the bottom centre between the zones from 720 px (under the book where the stage fits the room, else held 12 px over the box's bottom); above the book under the bar on phones, and where the lane between the zones is too narrow while the guide listens. The contents links are buttons that scroll the box (no `#fragment`); a page that scrolls inside itself is fenced too.
     - **canvas:** the stage is the box's room, dragged, swiped and panned with the window and the box still; Tab from card to card, a card brought to a spot clear of the corners; two columns on a phone; the preview in the box (§7).

6. **The guard, the model, the guide, the trail.**
   - **Guard (the seam owns its listeners).** The first draft gave renderers a `listen()` built on `guard.listen`. Stage 1 found that `guard.listen` keeps a clean-up per call for the guard's whole life, so a listener added and removed many times leaked a clean-up each time. As built, the seam's `on()` wraps each handler with the guard's `wrap`, adds and removes the listener itself, and keeps its own `off`; `configure()` releases them all, and a `listen` passed to `configure()` is ignored. The shell calls `configure({ scroller, wrap: guard.wrap, copy })` and **must call `configure()` from its destroy on `guard.onStop`**: that is what removes the seam's listeners when the layer fails. Order: configure, render, mount; Original: unmount, then `configure()`; Reimagined: `configure({...})`, then mount; destroy: unmount, then `configure()`. ## Rejected options

- **A full-screen sheet fixed under the header.** It covers Vodafone's footer and needs the page's scroll stopped, which reverses ADR-0046 §2, ADR-0064 §1 and plan §3.3, and risks the 15 px shift that ADR-0075 §4 fixed.
- **Re-implementing pinning per experience with `position: fixed`.** Ten experiences, each fragile; the scroller seam does it once.
- **Shipping only the experiences that do not pin** (magazine, flipbook, canvas). Too thin for a demo.
- **Gemini on live pages.** Against the owner's decision of 2026-11-10. Templates only.
- **Lifting Vodafone's accessibility button and the guide's mic for the view** (ADR-0044's way). Forbidden on live pages (AGENTS.md §4.6, ADR-0064 §1).
- **Mapping the experiences' colours to Vodafone's tokens.** Not chosen by the owner; kept as the way back (see Revisit when).
- **A fixed demo list of pages.** Not chosen; the gate decides (§4).
- **The preview helper inside `shared.js`** (phase 4b). It put the shell at 25,893 B, over its budget; `view-dialog.js` is a chunk of its own instead (§5).
- **Keeping fan's current card pressable while it moves** (phase 4b review). A moving card crosses the box's sides; `reveal()` places the cards at once instead, so the revealed card is current and pressable on return.

## Revisit when

- **The colour exception (§9):** before the demos reach visitors outside the owner's demos, or when Vodafone's brand team reviews them. Then decide: keep, map to Vodafone's tokens, or drop the experiences that do not fit.
- **The box (§1):** if visitors find two scrolls confusing, especially on phones; then try the page's scroll alone for short views, or a full-height box with the header's room reduced.
- Vodafone adds a control in either bottom corner, or moves the accessibility button or the mic: the safe zones move.
- Vodafone's themes stop setting `overflow-x: hidden` on `html` and `body`, so sticky pins again: the box may not be needed.
- Vodafone changes the header's pinning or a page's markup so the gate's result changes for the four demo pages.
- A page that passes the gate gives a poor view on the real site (coverage measured live, O29).
- The core, launcher, shell, any experience chunk or the Pocket passes its budget.
- ADR-0075 is not accepted, or changes §2, §3 or §8.
- The clone moves to the same box (phase 9): the seam's two settings merge.
- The Arabic review finds more than microcopy.
- A template is added, a name grows, or the composer's spacing changes: the shortest beside column (219 px at 812x375 from "Change experience") is only 4 px over `H_MIN`; re-measure before it drops below.
- The merged tree already has an ADR numbered 0076: renumber this one.
- Vodafone's sign-in card (already a hide reason, B-69), or any other element, takes the launcher's place on a page that passes the gate (the hide rules list the ones known).
