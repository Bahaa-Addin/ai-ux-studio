# ADR-0052: On Vodafone's pages the Pocket is a chunk the layer loads at idle; its album is a region of its own, the page turn freezes one copy of `#wrapper`, and everything it adds is marked, guarded and taken away

- Status: Proposed (2026-11-13). Part 1, the album, is built (phase 3 step S5); part 2, the "Pocket it" chips (§10-§14), is built (step S6). Accepted with phase 3's checks on live pages
- Date: 2026-11-13
- Area: the Pocket (experience layer), loading, build, failure isolation; live plan phase 3 (`docs/handoff/live-demos-way2.md` at the repository root), under ADR-0046 §2 and §4 and ADR-0048 §3 and §6–7; amends ADR-0048 §3 (how a demo's CSS reaches the page; a budget per chunk)
- Owner decisions: 2026-11-13, the Pocket keeps a sticker's copy as published and dated (ADR-0051); the album is on every page the layer runs on, chips only where coverage is 0.6 or more (phase 3 map); chips always visible and out of the Tab order (OD-1); for S6: the **screen-reader route only** (no keyboard shortcut, no key handled on Vodafone's page, so a sighted keyboard user cannot pocket), the chip is **Vodafone's secondary button**, and a card with no free corner gets an **icon-only chip in a corner**, counted as unplaced

## Context

Read and measured on 2026-11-13 (`docs/reference/live-way2-phase3-map-2026-11-13.md`, "Unsafe on a live page as built"). The Pocket was written for the Services clone, and on a live page it would have:

- appended the album inside `#main-content`, which its own page turn then hides, and frozen `.page-shell`, which does not exist on Vodafone's pages, so opening the Pocket threw with `state.busy` left true;
- thrown on a sticker without a picture (65 of 155 cards on the saved pages, all 20 bundles) *after* hiding `#main-content`, leaving Vodafone's content hidden with no way back;
- added layers, chips and a status line without `data-aixc`, through 17 raw listeners, timers and animation frames outside the guard, with a `destroy()` that removed only the mascot and the portal, so a guard stop could not leave the page as Vodafone served it;
- cloned the page twice per turn (5,416 nodes on the bundles page) and switched its lazy pictures to eager, fetching pictures from Vodafone for a copy shown for a second;
- replaced the accessibility widget's own `transition` on every page and lifted the button by a rule that could put it on the Arabic cookie banner (AGENTS.md 4.6);
- loaded the mascot (47 KB gzipped) and the portal (15 KB) with it, and needed `.aixc-sr-only` from the composer's stylesheet, which live pages do not load.

## Decision

1. **A demo is a chunk the core loads at idle.** For each demo the loader's tag names (`data-aixc-demos`) and the core knows, the core imports `host/demos/<name>.js` when the page is idle (`requestIdleCallback`, at most 2 s), through the guard, and calls its `start({ host, adapter, guard, layer, ensureCss, checkpoint })`. A demo's CSS is imported as text, minified at build time, and put on the page once by `ensureCss(name, text)`: one `<style data-aixc data-vg data-aixc-css="name">` in `<head>`, which a stop removes with the rest. The build gives each chunk a budget of its own, by the source module it is the entry of (`tests/unit/sdk-size.test.ts`), and checks that no output still refers to a file beside a module (`new URL('./…', import.meta.url)` would 404 on the gateway). The plugin that turns such references into `data:` URLs (ADR-0048 §3) waits for the first chunk that needs one: the mascot (S12) or the portal (S13). (Amends ADR-0048 §3.)
2. **The Pocket on every layer page.** `host/demos/pocket.js` starts the Pocket wherever the layer runs. Its album shows stickers from every page; where no card can be pocketed (coverage under 0.6, or no model) it is the album alone.
3. **The Pocket takes its host as seams** (`experiences/src/pocket/pocket.js`): where the album goes (`place`), what the turn freezes (`snapshotRoot`) and from how far down (`sheetTop`), how the page's content is covered and given back, also in the frozen copy (`cover` returns `restore` and `inCopy`), how a node is marked (`mark`), how a handler, timer or frame is guarded (`wrap`), test checkpoints, labels, the page, and switches (`corner`, `chips`, `dated`, `eagerImages`, `pageBack`). The clone's shell supplies today's behaviour by default. Its store, its mascot (`createActors`) and its portal (`createPortal`) are passed in; the clone passes all three, the live chunk passes the store alone.
4. **The album is a region of its own.** `PageHost.addRegion` puts it after `#experience-output`, as `#pocket-output` (so `pocket.css` matches), marked `data-aixc` and `data-vg`. While it shows, `#main-content` is hidden through `host.hideSource()` and the layer's own regions are hidden; closing gives each back the state it had (a reimagined view keeps Vodafone's content hidden).
5. **One quiet copy per turn.** The turn freezes `#wrapper` (else `<body>`) below whatever Vodafone pins at the top (`host.chromeBottom()`). The page's back face shows nothing of its front (`seeThrough: 0`), so one copy is made per turn. The copy has no ids, none of the layer's tags (`data-aixc-*`), no frames, videos, audio, plugins, scripts, stylesheet links, `noscript` or `template` (a frame or video becomes an empty box of its size), and its lazy pictures stay lazy: nothing is fetched for it.
6. **Everything is marked, guarded and taken away.** Every node the Pocket puts in `<body>` and its region carry `data-aixc` and `data-vg`. Every listener, timer and animation frame runs through the guard (`wrap`). Opening and closing give the page back if anything fails part way (the album hidden, Vodafone's content and the layer's regions restored, the scroll position back), then pass the error to the guard, which stops the layer. `destroy()` cancels every frame and timer, removes every listener, layer, region and class, gives the page back, and is registered with `guard.onStop`. Test checkpoints: `pocket-open`, `pocket-render`, `pocket-close`.
7. **Stickers are shown as they were kept** (ADR-0051): the copy as saved, the section's title, the picture or none; a sticker kept in the other language marks its own words with that language and direction; on Vodafone's pages each sticker says the day it was saved ("Saved 12 Nov", "اتحفظت 12 نوفمبر" with Latin digits, as Vodafone writes its prices). Announcements go through the page's language. "Back to the page" replaces the clone's "Back to Services" on live pages. The fonts are variables, with Vodafone's Arabic face names first on Arabic pages (the theme names them apart). The album keeps its own column and a full screen of height there, since the themes' `.container` differs or is missing.
8. **What part 1 leaves out on live pages:** the "Pocket it" chips (S6, part 2 of this ADR, §10-§14), the page's corner and the Pocket button (S11, under the owner's accessibility-button exception, its ADR numbered when written), the mascot and drone (S12), the portal (S13) and the journey (S10). Until S11, nothing on a live page opens the album but the layer's own call (`window.__aixc.demos.pocket.open()` with `?aixc-debug`), and a pocketed card peels in place: no sticker flies before the corner it would fly to exists.
9. **The clone keeps working as it did.** It keeps its ids-only store behind the same interface (`experiences/src/pocket/ids-store.js`) until phase 9. The accessibility-button rules in `pocket.css` now match only on the clone's page (`:where(body:has(.page-shell))`), with the same weight as before: a first version that raised their weight let the open Pocket's rule beat the portal's, which keeps the button above the transition (caught by `portal_e2e.py` before commit). It keeps its chips inside its cards (`chips: true`); the live chunk passes `chips: 'overlay'` and the overlay's factory (`createChips`, `experiences/src/pocket/chips-overlay.js`), as it passes the store, so the clone never loads the overlay.

### Part 2: "Pocket it" on Vodafone's cards (S6)

10. **An overlay of our own; nothing written into Vodafone's cards.** The chips live in one region, `div.pocket-chips` (`data-aixc data-vg`, `role=group`, named "Pocket it on this page" in the page's language, the page's `lang` and `dir`), put by `PageHost.addRegion` after the layer's other regions: outside `#main-content`, before the footer, `position: relative; height: 0; z-index: 2`. Each marked card gets a slot of its own in it, absolutely placed over the card (`translate` from the two boxes, so right-to-left scrolling and body offsets do not matter), the card's size and corner radius, holding the chip, the liner and the lift; `pocket.js` `decorate(card, id, host)` draws them on the slot exactly as the clone draws them on its cards, and the clone's state classes go on the slot. A slot takes no pointer (`pointer-events: none`) except on its chip and on the liner of a pocketed card, so every other press reaches Vodafone's card. Nothing is written into Vodafone's elements beyond ADR-0049's two marks, no chip is inside a link (so pressing one never navigates), and the adapter never sees the region (it watches `#main-content` only). z-index 2 puts the chips above Owl's carousels (z 1) and under Vodafone's navigation (3, 20 pinned), its sticky banner (7), its dialogs (99), OneTrust (2147483645) and the accessibility widget (2147483647), so AGENTS 4.6 holds by construction. The lift sits in the slot rather than in a fixed layer: it keeps the page's stacking, scrolls with the page without script, and is cut with its slot. Rejected: a fixed layer redrawn on every scroll (a frame behind on every move), CSS anchor positioning (not in every browser we test), chips inside the cards (they would add Tab stops, carry English on Arabic pages and press through the card's link).
11. **Which cards and when.** The adapter's marked cards (`[data-aixc-item]` under `#main-content`) that the current model knows, the first element per id, never inside Owl's loop copies (`.owl-item.cloned`) or anything of ours. They are found again when the page is read again (`aixc:model` → `pocket.setModel(model, coverage)`, which refills the catalog in place) or when a mark moves (a mutation observer on `#main-content` for `data-aixc-item`, `style`, `class` and `hidden`; any other change only places the chips again). A pass places every chip, all reads first, then all writes, on the next frame after: a resize of a card or of `<body>`, a window resize, fonts loading, a scroll inside the page (Owl's stage when focus moves into a hidden slide), and while a transition runs in the content (Owl's stage, the home slides; followed for up to 1 s). The page's own scroll needs nothing. A slot is hidden while its card has no box, is not visible, or lies wholly outside the ancestors that cut it (`overflow` other than `visible`: Owl's stage, horizontal lists); a card partly cut is cut the same way (`clip-path: inset(...)`, which cuts the lift and hit-testing too). Chips show only on the website (`siteOf(location.hostname) === 'web'`; the shop's come with E16) where the reading covered at least 0.6 of the content, checked on every new reading; the region is hidden while the album shows or turns, and while the layer hides Vodafone's content.
12. **Where on the card, and how it looks.** A pure module (`experiences/src/pocket/chips-place.js`) tries the corners in this order: top end, bottom end, top start, bottom start (the end is the right in English, the left in Arabic), 10 px in (8 under 640 px wide). A corner is taken when the chip lies inside the card and meets none of: the words of the card's headings and of its plain links (their line boxes: a heading's box often spans the card while its words do not), its smaller buttons that show a box of their own (a fill or a border), and its smaller pictures (icons, logos), "smaller" meaning under half the card's area, so a card that is itself a link is never avoided. The full chip is tried first, then the icon alone (32×32, its name unchanged for screen readers). Where nothing fits, the icon goes to the top end corner and the card is counted as unplaced (`__aixc.demos.pocket.chips().unplaced`, ids only). The corner is picked again only when the card's size or the fonts change, never while scrolling or while the stage moves. The chip is Vodafone's secondary button (SPEC-01 §3), on live pages only (scoped to `.pocket-chips[data-aixc]`): white, a 1 px #e60000 border, #e60000 text in Vodafone Regular at 14 px, 6 px corners, filled #e60000 with white text on hover, a two-tone focus ring (#333 outline 2 px out, white between), no transitions under reduced motion; the liner is #f4f4f4 with a #ccc dashed border. The clone keeps its own look until phase 9. Known: the lift peels from the top right corner, as on the clone, wherever the chip is.
13. **Keyboard: the screen-reader route only** (owner, 2026-11-13). Every chip and Put back is a `<button type=button tabindex="-1">`, so the Tab sequence is exactly the one `?aixc=off` gives: no stop added, none taken. Screen readers meet the buttons by name ("Pocket it: Vodafone Cash", "في الجيب: ڤودافون كاش"; Put back's name starts with its visible label, "Put back …", "رجّعها: …", WCAG 2.5.3) in the labelled group after the content, in card order. Pressing a chip with focus on it moves focus to Put back, and back again, as on the clone. From our own buttons only, Escape and Shift+Tab go back to the card's own link (the card if it is one), and Tab goes on to what follows the card on Vodafone's page; no key is handled anywhere on Vodafone's page and no shortcut exists. A sighted keyboard user cannot pocket a card. The map's "Tab hands over to it" and the plan's Alt+Shift+P (with its spoken hint) are not built. Known: the Great Entertainment cards are links without `href` on Vodafone's page, so they are not focusable there either; Escape from their chips goes to the stop before the card.
14. **Records per page, and smaller fixes.** A card shows as pocketed only by a record kept on this page (`onPage(record, page)`: the same page key and site, in either language), so a sticker kept on another page with the same card id never marks this page's card. A live record keeps its own key (`recordKey: null` on live; the clone's ids-only store keys a sticker by its card's id, `recordKey: (id) => id`): without it, live records took the card id as their key, which S6's tests caught before any live sticker existed. Step S7 replaces the id match with `rematch()`. While focus is inside a pocketed card (its own link under the liner), the liner turns see-through (opacity 0.35), so the focused link is never hidden (WCAG 2.4.11). The lift's and the flight's copies of a card no longer keep the adapter's marks (B-49), the lift's and the flight's hosts are hidden from assistive technology (`aria-hidden`, `inert`), and the two announcements of pocketing and putting back are in the page's language (B-48). Test checkpoints: `pocket-chips` (every build and placement pass), `pocket-chip` (before a chip or Put back does anything).

## Consequences

- The album works on every saved CMS page in both languages, with stickers from any page, without a picture, and in the other language.
- A failure anywhere in it leaves the page as with `?aixc=off`; nothing reaches the page's own error handlers.
- A turn costs one copy of `#wrapper` and no request.
- The core grows by the loader and `ensureCss`: 7,647 bytes gzipped (budget raised to 7.75 KB). The Pocket chunk is 24,830 bytes gzipped (budget 26 KB), without the mascot, the drone or the portal; up to a third of it is the UI copy of every demo in Arabic (`i18n.js`, 8,155 bytes gzipped on its own), which a later step can split by demo.
- Until S11 the album has no visible entry on live pages.
- S6: chips on every card a person can see on the saved pages, with no Tab stop added and nothing written into Vodafone's cards; one pass per frame at most, none while the page itself scrolls. Chips appear when the Pocket's chunk has loaded (at idle, up to 2 s). The core is unchanged (7,840 bytes gzipped). The Pocket chunk is 29,287 bytes gzipped (budget raised to 29.25 KB): the overlay, its placement and observers cost about 3.9 KB, more than the 1.5-2.5 KB estimated; a chunk of its own for the chips, loaded only where the gate lets them show, is the follow-up.
- A sighted keyboard user cannot pocket a card (the owner's choice); a screen-reader user can, by name.

## Pilot knobs

- The idle wait before a demo loads (2 s at most, `host/index.js`).
- The page's back face (`PAGE_BACK` in `host/demos/pocket.js`), the labels and `dated`.
- The per-chunk budgets (`tests/unit/sdk-size.test.ts`).
- S6: the coverage gate (`CHIPS_COVERAGE`, 0.6) and chips per site (website only); the corner order, the insets (10 px, 8 under 640 px), the "under half the card" rule for what a chip avoids, and the icon-only fallback; the overlay's z-index (2); how long the chips follow a transition (1 s); the liner's see-through opacity (0.35); no flight before S11.

## Revisit when

- The layer moves to Vodafone's tag manager (Mode A): when a demo may load, and under which OneTrust category.
- Vodafone pins more of its page than `#header` and `#nav-header` (`host.chromeBottom()`).
- The UI copy grows: split `i18n.js` by demo.
- S11 gives the Pocket its corner on the page (a sticker can fly again; a second way in for keyboard users); S7 matches records to cards with `rematch()`; phase 9 removes the clone's in-card chips; E16 brings chips to the shop.
- The owner sees the chips on live pages or reconsiders the keyboard (a shortcut, or chips in the Tab order); a screen-reader test finds the group's place or order confusing.
- The Pocket chunk passes its budget again: split the chips into a chunk of their own.
