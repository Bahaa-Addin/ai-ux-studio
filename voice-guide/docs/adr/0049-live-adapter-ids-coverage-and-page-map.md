# ADR-0049: The live adapter keys what it reads by stable ids, counts the page's own dialogs as actions, and keeps each section's knowledge destination on the model (from `GET /v1/page-map`), never as a tag on Vodafone's portlets; the crawler starts locators at the portlet

- Status: Proposed (2026-11-11); Accepted when the live adapter's checks pass on the live pages (blocked by O29), like ADR-0048
- Date: 2026-11-11
- Area: experience layer, knowledge service, gateway; phase 2 of the live plan (`docs/handoff/live-demos-way2.md` at the repository root), under ADR-0046 §3. Amends ADR-0046 §3 on three points: item ids (the first unique candidate, not the link alone), coverage (the page's own dialogs count as actions), and the knowledge link (the layer does **not** put `data-vg-destination` on Vodafone's elements)

## Context

ADR-0046 §3 has a live adapter read Vodafone's page into the content model the demos already use, key items by their normalised link, and tag what it read, including `data-vg-destination` from the knowledge base, so the guide spotlights the same card whichever view shows. Phase 2 built the adapter (`experiences/src/host/live-adapter.js`, `recognisers.js`) and read the saved pages of 2026-10-23 with it (`tests/integration/live-adapter.test.ts`, runbook §3b). What it found:

- **Links do not make unique ids.** Six Shokran cards on `/en/services` share one link; every bundle on `/en/mobile-internet-bundles` links `/spa/miManagement` (O26). Titles differ between English and Arabic. Pictures are served as `/documents/<ids>/<file name>/<CMS UUID>?t=…`, where the UUID changes when an editor re-uploads.
- **Some cards have no link but work.** The seven Great Entertainment cards on `/en/services` open one of the page's own dialogs (`data-toggle="modal"`, "Download Ana Vodafone"). Counting only links put the page's coverage at 0.76, under Reimagine's threshold, on a page that works.
- **A destination tag on a portlet hides finer targets.** The trail's resolver (`packages/sdk/src/trail/resolver.ts`) takes the nearest `[data-vg-destination]` ancestor first, before the destinations' own locators (deepest match wins there). A section tag on a whole portlet would make every click inside it the section, and hide the cards and pictures the knowledge base locates more precisely.
- **`GET /v1/experience` cannot serve the layer.** It answers 404 while `trail.enabled` is off (the live site config has it off) and carries the trail's questions and profile, which the layer does not need.
- **The knowledge base's section locators are positional** (`#layout-column_column-1 > div.portlet-boundary.portlet-static:nth-of-type(4) > …`, O26), and some labels came from a dialog inside the section rather than the section's own title.

## Decision

1. **Sections are portlets, keyed by instance id.** A section is a Liferay portlet in `#main-content`; its id is the portlet's instance id in lower case (`…_INSTANCE_w9Jro8TDb1vH_` → `w9jro8tdb1vh`), the same across days and languages.
2. **Item ids: the first unique candidate.** For each card, in order: its link's path (without `/en` or `/ar`), its picture's file name (CMS UUID and `+` removed), its title as shown (its Latin letters and digits only, so an Arabic title gives another id or none), its tab and title, its place in the section. The first candidate that is unique on the page wins. Owl's copies (`.owl-item.cloned`), dialogs, FAQ entries and the like are never read.
3. **Coverage counts the page's own dialogs, and what no row read.** A card is usable when it has a title and an action: a link, or a control that opens one of the page's own dialogs. Such a card's call to action is `{ label, href: null, opens: 'dialog' }`; no link is invented. Coverage is usable cards over everything that looks like a card: the cards a recogniser row matched, plus card-like blocks no row read (`coverage.unread`): each thing a person can press that no read card holds is grown to the largest block holding only it, and the block counts when it has words and a picture, a heading or a button. A portlet with no read card and one such block is a hero or a promo band, as before; a card a row leaves out on purpose is not unread. A demo is offered on a page only above its threshold (Reimagine 0.9, Pocket 0.6). Links and pictures are resolved as the browser does, against the page's own address (with its query) or its `<base>`. (Amended 2026-11-12: unread blocks were not counted at first, so a renamed class left the ratio at 1; B-30.)
4. **The knowledge destination stays on the model.** The layer asks the gateway `GET /v1/page-map?url=<origin + path>` (header `x-vg-site-key`; the query and fragment of the page's address are never sent) and sets `section.destinationId` on each section it can match: a destination's first locator that finds exactly one element, inside one portlet. Where several land on the same portlet: `section` destinations before `collection` and `faq` ones; among those, the one whose title is the section's (only when both have one); else the innermost, so a locator for the whole portlet gives way to one for the section inside it (as the trail's resolver goes deepest first). Locators that rely on `data-vg-destination` are not used (the knowledge base has none today). **Nothing is tagged on Vodafone's portlets for this**; the guide and the trail keep resolving the page's real elements by the knowledge base's own locators. Views the layer draws itself (Reimagine's templates, phase 4) put `data-vg-destination` on their own elements, where no finer target exists.
5. **`GET /v1/page-map`.** A knowledge handler (`pageMap`) returns `{ kbVersionId, pageId, sections: [{ id, kind, css, title }] }` for the page: section, collection and FAQ destinations with a target and no commit point, at most 100, titles only (no page values). The gateway serves it only when the site's `experiences.enabled` is on (404 `experiences_off` otherwise), not gated on the trail, with `Cache-Control: private, max-age=300`. The demo browser's loader passes the public site key to the layer as `data-aixc-site-key`. The request carries no referrer. If the map does not come within 2 s, or fails, the page is read without it; `window.__aixc.pageMap` (with `?aixc-debug`) is then `null`.
6. **The crawler starts locators at the portlet (phase 2c).** `jobs/crawler/extract.ts` treats a Liferay portlet id (`p_p_id_<portlet>_INSTANCE_<id>_`) as a stable id whatever its length or digits, so a locator inside a portlet starts there (`#p_p_id_…_INSTANCE_w9Jro8TDb1vH_ > section.portlet > …`), not at the column's nth portlet (`#layout-column_column-1 > div.portlet-boundary.portlet-static:nth-of-type(3) > …`), which points at another section once an editor adds one above. An element's own stable id still comes first (`#test`). Portlet ids are the same in English and Arabic, so the builder still joins a section's two languages by its first locator. The builder (`jobs/kb-builder/build.ts`) never names a destination after a portlet id: a card list whose locator starts at one is named as a section is, after its English heading (else a hash of its locator); before, it took the id its locator started at, which was the column's (`web.wireless-net.layout-column_column-1`).

7. **The page is read again when it changes (2026-11-12).** `watch()` re-reads 250 ms after Vodafone's content under `#main-content` changes, elements or words (carousels, lazy sections, a new price; not what happens inside the layer's own nodes), at the latest 2 s after the first change not yet read, with the same ids for the same cards. `tag()` marks what was read and takes the layer's marks off everything else, so a carousel's copies, which are made from the cards' markup, do not keep them; `untag()` sweeps the page. The layer keeps its model current and sends `aixc:model` on the document when it changed. This is the live plan's phase 2 step 1, built after the review found it missing (B-31).
8. **The layer's own nodes are never read, and each card says what it can be known by (2026-11-13, phase 3).** Everything the layer adds carries `data-aixc`, and the recognisers skip it (`SKIP` in `recognisers.js`), so the Pocket's chips and its lifted copy of a card are neither cards nor unread buttons: before this, one lifted copy on the home page turned `vodafone-red1` into two made-up ids, and the chips took the Vodafone Cash band's tag away. Each item also carries `kind` (the recogniser row) and `keys`, what the card can be known by: its link's path without `/en` or `/ar`, its picture's file name, its title as shown, the tab it sits in, and the numbers in its title. The id stays the first unique candidate (§2); the keys let a saved sticker find its card again when that id changes, in the other language, or on another date (ADR-0051).

## Consequences

- On the saved `/en/services` and `/ar/services` every one of the six sections gets the destination the clone mapped by hand (`experiences/src/main.js` `GUIDE_DESTINATIONS`), including Great Entertainment, whose portlet also holds a section the knowledge base named after its dialog. Every saved page gets the same destinations with its section headings reworded or removed.
- Item ids are the same in English and Arabic on Services (28 of 30; the two others are slips on Vodafone's Arabic page: Mini Call links `/spa/roaming`, one picture has another name), home (8 of 8), RED (5 of 5) and Vodafone Cash (8 of 8), but only 2 of 20 on the bundles page, whose cards share one link (`/spa/miManagement`) and no picture tells them apart, so their ids come from the titles, English on one page and Arabic on the other. A Pocket item saved there in one language is not the same item in the other (O26).
- A page where Vodafone renamed a class loses coverage and the demo is not offered there, instead of a broken view: every card kind renamed on every saved page it is on (19 cases) falls under 0.9; with the 17 service cards of `/en/services` renamed, coverage is 0.41 and the three sections are named as unread. The home-wireless page, whose router offer and price table no row reads, stays at 0.
- Counting unread blocks found content no row read: the home page's banner slides and the RED page's picture rows. Each has a recogniser row now (home reads 10 items, RED 8; no earlier id changed), so every saved page with cards is still at 1.
- The core script is 7,072 bytes gzipped (budget 7.5 KB), up from 5,343 bytes, for the unread count, the two rows and the re-read. Reading a saved page takes 1 to 9 ms.
- The guide and the trail behave exactly as without the layer on Vodafone's elements: the layer adds only `data-aixc-section` and `data-aixc-item`, and takes them off when it stops.
- Until the next sync the committed knowledge base keeps its positional locators: when an editor adds a section above, such a locator finds nothing or another portlet's section, and the matcher names that section wrongly or not at all. After it, locators start at the portlet (§6).
- The next `npm run sync` changes most destinations, and the churn check (15%) holds it for review. Its reports pair each renamed id with its new one by a label of its own, list apart what could only be paired by its place (to be checked on the site) and what has no counterpart (`jobs/kb-diff.ts`, B-33), so a place really lost is not hidden among the renames. Measured on the saved pages (the 8 knowledge-base pages in the replay; old crawler and builder against new, pictures described from their metadata as a sync does, the curated overlay, the site's stylesheets not served): 170 of 309 destinations change, 144 keep their names with new locators and 26 get new names: 4 card lists named after the column and their 16 items take their headings (`…layout-column_column-1…` → `web.wireless-net.home-wireless-bundles…`, `web.vodafone-red1.red-exclusive…`), and 2 sections and 4 pictures named after a hash get a new one. Without pictures: 52 of 106, 22 renamed. Estimated for the whole committed knowledge base (`20261023-122606-5b2080`) by the same rules, assuming every portlet carries its Liferay id as on the saved pages: 356 of 524 destinations (68%) have a locator through a portlet; 126 of them get new names and 230 keep their names. The 126: 44 named after a hash of their locator (16 sections, 28 pictures) get a new hash; 11 card lists named after the column and their 71 items take their English heading (7 lists, 32 items) or, without one, a hash (4 lists, 39 items: short numbers, Kart el Korout and two on an Arabic-only page). The site experience profile names all 126, so rebuild it after activating. Names made this way stay the same on later syncs, as long as the portlets and headings do.
- One more request per page view to the gateway. A browser under the tag manager may keep it five minutes; the demo browser asks every time, since it routes `/__vg/**` itself and Chromium keeps no cache for routed requests.

## Verified (2026-11-11, owner's Mac, saved pages of 2026-10-23)

- `tests/integration/live-adapter.test.ts`: every saved CMS page read with unique, valid ids and nothing dropped (the runbook §3b table); `/en/services` in detail (8 portlets, 12 action cards, 17 service cards, hero, app band, coverage 1, dialog cards without invented links); ids across languages; Owl copies never read and reading changes nothing; the six Services sections' destinations in both languages, with nothing tagged.
- `tests/integration/experience.test.ts`: `GET /v1/page-map` is 404 `experiences_off` on a site without the layer; with it, the page's section destinations with locators and titles only, never a commit point; an unknown page gets none.
- `tests/e2e/aixc-layer.e2e.test.ts`: through the demo browser and the real gateway, the layer on `/en/services` and `/ar/services` names all six sections.
- `tests/unit/aixc-delivery.test.ts`: the loader puts the site key on the layer's tag.
- `tests/integration/live-adapter.test.ts` also: Vodafone rewords or drops a heading the knowledge base still has, and each section keeps its destination (Services and the bundles page, both languages; it failed with the first matcher, which took the dialog's destination).
- `tests/e2e/aixc-layer.e2e.test.ts` also: the request carries only the page's address (no query, no fragment) even from `?…&msisdn=…#…`; a 500, a 404, an answer that is not JSON, no network, or no answer within 2 s: the page is read all the same, without the map and without a warning.
- `tests/integration/crawler-portlets.test.ts` (phase 2c): on the saved Services pages every portlet section is located from its portlet or its own id, never by position, in both languages alike, as are the pictures, buttons and (RED, home wireless) card lists inside portlets; with a new portlet added at the top of the column every such locator finds the same section, while the positional ones the crawler made before find another; the builder keeps the portlet locator first, joins English and Arabic, names no destination after a portlet id and names card lists after their headings. All three fail with the crawler as it was; the last also with the builder as it was (it named 20 destinations `…p_p_id_…_instance_…_`, found by the review).
- 2026-11-12, after the independent review and a review of its fixes (B-29 to B-33; Opus on masking and the sync report, Sonnet on the rest; 9 further findings, all fixed with tests): links resolved against the page (four saved pages, a `<base>`, a fragment link); coverage with a renamed class; re-reading and mark sweeping in the adapter and in the real layer; `tests/unit/kb-diff.test.ts` and the sync test for the paired report.
- Not verified: the live pages (O29), where Owl's own script makes the copies and the 38 knowledge-base pages that are not in the replay (34 of them `/en/` or `/ar/` pages).

## Pilot knobs

- The coverage thresholds per demo (Reimagine 0.9, Pocket 0.6), set where each demo is offered (phases 3 and 4).
- The page map's time limit in the layer (2 s, `experiences/src/host/index.js`) and its cache header (300 s, `services/gateway/server.ts`).
- The recognisers' rows (`experiences/src/host/recognisers.js`), one per Vodafone component.
- The re-read delay (250 ms, `watch` in `live-adapter.js`) and what counts as a card-like block (`unreadCards`).

## Revisit when

- The trail's resolver changes how it treats `data-vg-destination` (a tag that only adds, never hides, would allow tagging the real portlets).
- The first sync with portlet locators is activated: check the title tie-break still decides something, else drop it.
- Vodafone leaves Liferay, or its portlet ids start changing between publishes.
- A demo keeps Pocket items across languages on pages like the bundles page: ids need a key Vodafone gives both languages (O26).
- The layer moves to Vodafone's tag manager (Mode A): the site key reaches the layer another way than the demo browser's `data-aixc-site-key`.
