Voice guide preview

An independent prototype. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with Vodafone. It adds a voice guide and a few demo experiences to the site's public pages, in this browser only.

Questions you ask go to the prototype's server to be answered. In browsers without built-in speech recognition, your voice is sent to the prototype's speech-to-text service outside Egypt, after a notice, and deleted once it is written as text.
