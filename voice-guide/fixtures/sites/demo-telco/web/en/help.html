Help

How do I check my balance?

Dial *868*1# or open My account to see your remaining data and balance.

How do I recharge?

Recharge with a mobile wallet, a scratch card, or at any Demo Telco store.

How do I stop auto-renewal?

Open My account, choose your bundle, then turn off auto-renewal.

How do I activate roaming?

Roaming is active on all postpaid lines. Prepaid customers dial *868*5# before travelling.
